# src/shared — battle rules (no DOM, no Three)

Applies together with the root `CLAUDE.md`.

- **Content lives in `src/content`** (Nov 14, Fernando's decision): classes, abilities, items and maps are data there (see its `CLAUDE.md`); `units.ts`, `abilities.ts`, `equipment.ts` and `scenarios.ts` here keep the types and build the registries. The maps named below (`dressBeaconHill`, `CRYPT_KEY`, the `*_H` and `*_RAMPS` drawings) are in `src/content/maps/`. Nothing here imports `src/client`.
- **State is plain data** (`BattleState`, JSON-friendly): saving, cloning, undo and simulation depend on it. The map and the definitions live outside the state (the state keeps only the scenario id).
- **A single class, `Battle`**, because it has a lifecycle (state, undo history, save). Everything else is pure functions and data; composition, never inheritance (Arthur's rule).
- **Randomness only through `rng.ts`** (`nextRng`, `rollInt`), with the RNG state inside `BattleState`. Never `Math.random` here. Order of rolls per target: hit, then damage. Undo restores everything except the RNG.
- **An ability is data with general stats** (range, damage, area, push, delay); no "ability X against Y" rules.
- **A unit or ability number is best changed after measuring** with `npm run sim` (record before/after in the comment or the commit). The simulator is optional: balance is decided by playing.
- **Trust boundary:** the save comes from the browser; `Battle.load`/`parseBattleState` rebuild only known fields and return `null` on any doubt.
- Scripts run with Node stripping types: no syntax that generates code (constructor parameter properties, `enum`).
- **FFT turn rules on every map** (Nov 12, Fernando's request): turn cost 100/80/60, tie-break by the list with enemies first, and actions with charge (`AbilityDef.charge`, `BattleState.charges`), sourced in `docs/medicoes/regras-fft-2026-11-12.md`. A strong ability is slow because of its charge, not because of a delay.
- **Water on every map** (Nov 12, Fernando's decision "like FFT", BMG 6.5): `Tile.depth` is optional; 1 = shallow, 2 or more = deep (`isDeep`). Entering water costs 1 point, like ground (Nov 13, Fernando's choice: fewer movement shackles; it used to cost 2). Allies pass through each other (you just can't stop on their tile); an enemy blocks. Since Nov 13 (Fernando's choice: "enters, but does not act inside"; in FFT only swimmers enter) deep water lets you enter and stop, and a unit in it can only walk or wait (`previewAbility` returns `water`). Shallow water does not lower anyone; in deep water the height of whoever is in it = `standH` (height minus depth), used for hit chance, line of sight, range and falls. The jump between tiles is measured by the surface (`t.h`), otherwise the bank would be 2 levels above the deep water. Movement, push, save and AI go through `canStand`/`stepCost`/`standH`/`isDeep` in `grid.ts`. A push stopped by deep water is our own assumption (kept on Nov 13). Report: `docs/medicoes/agua-2026-11-12.md`.
- **Per-tile look on the game maps** (Nov 12): `Tile.look` is only for drawing. Beacon Hill gets its own in `dressBeaconHill` (snow on the enemy side, rock on the hill, gold at the Beacon); the Flooded Crypt (`flooded_crypt`) is written with one letter per tile (`CRYPT_KEY`); since Nov 13 the Hill has a valley, a peak-5 hill and an enemy plateau at 3. New map: state the terrain, height and `look` of each tile and register the floor name in `LOOK_NAME` (`hud.ts`) and the pieces in `LOOK` (`scene.ts`). Report: `docs/medicoes/mapas-pecas-2026-11-12.md`.
- **Ramps on the game maps** (Nov 13, Fernando's request: "it's not Minecraft"): `Tile.ramp` is still drawing only (the rules don't read it; jump, range and sight follow `standH`), except where a half-tile token may stop (`restsOn`, below). Each campaign map carries an ASCII ramp drawing (`BEACON_RAMPS`, `WOOD_RAMPS`, `PASS_RAMPS`, `CRYPT_RAMPS`, `HEART_RAMPS`, applied by `withRamps` in `grid.ts`, which fails on a drawing of the wrong size), hand-picked on the passages and slopes with a step of 1; in the crypts the drawing is a staircase. `tests/scenario-maps.test.ts` guarantees every ramp is ground without water and rises exactly 1 level to a neighboring ground tile.
- **Crypt Heart** (`crypt_heart`, night of Nov 12, stage 4): 12x14 with the `CRYPT_KEY` letters (plus `W`, deep water) and per-tile height in `HEART_H` since Nov 13, the boss on an altar behind a deep moat that can only be crossed by 2 bridges; it has a Beacon.
- **Campaign maps and units** (night of Nov 12): Ash Wood (`ash_wood`, deep river with a bridge and a ford in a valley; since Nov 13 the height of each tile comes from `WOOD_H`, 5 levels, enemy plateau with drops of 2 on the descents) and Frost Pass (`frost_pass`, climb in steps of 1 up to the summit; since Nov 13 zigzagging, with the height of each tile in `PASS_H`), both without a Beacon (the campaign uses "last one standing wins"); Skeleton Minion (`minion`, fragile and fast) and the Shadow Wave (`shadow_wave`, radius 2, charge 6, the boss spell). Stage casts and lines live in `src/content/campaign.ts`.
- **Veil Acolyte** (`acolyte`, night of Nov 12): an enemy that heals (Fireball + Heal), so the stage asks for target priority; it uses the mage model with `UnitDef.tint` (violet emissive glow, drawing only, copied into the `dev-roster` sheets; the material color multiplied the texture and did not show on the dark model). In Frost Pass in place of the Shadow Mage: 51% → 39% (in place of the Minion it dropped to 8%).
- **Large unit** (night of Nov 12): `UnitDef.size` (2 = 2x2, `pos` is the top-left tile). Every "who is on the tile" question goes through `unitAt`/`footprint` (`pathing.ts`); range, sight, area and hit chance measure from the nearest tile (`nearestTile`, `closest`). A large unit does not walk (move 0) and is not pushed. For units that occupy 1 tile nothing changes (measured: Hill identical). Today only the Bone Colossus (`colossus`), outside the developer menu.
- **Half tiles in every game battle** (Nov 14, Fernando: "the half-tile movement mechanic still doesn't exist", looking at stage 0): the client opens every battle except the FFT study through `halve` (`half.ts`): the map split into cells (each tile becomes 2x2, `Grid.sub` = 2, the object drawn once by `Tile.span`), each unit a 2x2 token `<def>½` (size and move doubled, `baseDef`/`isObjective` read the original) that walks 1 cell at a time (`reachableBig`: deep water, a straddle across a step up to the jump, the gap) and stops only on even ground (`restsOn`, Nov 14, Fernando: "it must not let the character stand still on a corner like that"): the square's 4 cells share height, water depth, spikes and terrain, and a ramp cell is under it only on its own whole tile; it still walks over steps, ledges and ramps on the way. Every whole-tile stop stays a stop. Abilities stay written in whole tiles: `farCells`/`nearCells` turn range and radius into cells between the two squares, `aimEnds` measures range from the closest cells of the shooter's square and the aimed tile's (one of the Colossus' four, not its whole square), line of sight walks whole-tile strides from square corner to square corner and probes each 2x2 (`sightLine`, `sightEnds`), any cell aims at its whole tile (`aimTile`: the map's tile on empty ground, Nov 14 review), `areaOf` centers an area on the whole square of whoever stands there, `wholeTiles` gives the hit chance its distance, and the push moves `push x sub` cells checking the whole square. A push also ends only on even ground (`restsOn`, Nov 15, Fernando: "it tie-breaks by making the guy fall, or hit a wall"): a square left half over a drop goes 1 more cell and falls onto the lower tile (the usual fall damage); otherwise (a step up, spikes, other terrain or ground under a side, a unit or wall in the way) it goes back to where it last rested and takes the bump. Before, 6 to 16% of the 1-tile pushes from a stop between tiles ended hovering over a ledge corner (`tests/half-tiles.test.ts` pushes 1 to 3 tiles in 4 directions from every stop of every spawn on every map). On whole-tile positions every answer equals the whole map's (`tests/half-tiles.test.ts`: ranges, targets, hit chance and side for every ability, the shots of every sighted ability on every game map, walking from every spawn of every map, push). The AI counts its per-tile weights back in tiles. Whole maps (tests, simulator, FFT study) are unchanged.
- **Spikes** (night of Nov 12, our own rule): `Tile.spikes`; being pushed onto the tile (`simulatePush`) or ending your turn on it (`spikeAtTurnEnd`) hurts `COMBAT.SPIKE_DAMAGE` (10), `spike` event; walking over it does not hurt. The AI avoids stopping there and values pushing onto it for the damage. Today only in the Flooded Crypt (letter `s`).
- **AI and charge** (Nov 12): the Aimed Shot (a charge that aims at the tile, `panel`) is worth 40% against whoever plays before it fires (`W.ESCAPE`); a spell with charge follows the unit and gets no discount.
- **Jump by class** (Nov 13, Fernando's request: "jump mechanics like FFT"): `UnitDef.jump` (without it, `MAX_JUMP` = 1) says how many levels the unit climbs or descends in one step; every jump question goes through `jumpOf` (`pathing.ts`), which the FFT study swaps for `rules.jump`. Rogue and Minion 2, the rest 1 (in FFT proportion, where the Thief jumps 1 more than the other classes; with Rogue 3 and archers 2, stage 5 ranged from 35% to 77%). FFT gap (`leaps`, same day, Fernando's request): flies up to `[Jump/2]` tiles in a straight line over deep water or tiles lower than both ends (a wall blocks), 1 movement point per tile flown; the AI sees it in `walkMap`. With jump 2 the Rogue crosses the moat of the Crypt Heart; in the Gariland study (jump 3 for every class) the gap also counts, and the battle ended at 73% (300 seeds). A push still hits a step of 2 (`MAX_JUMP`, it is physics, not the jump of whoever is pushed).
- **The AI measures distance on foot** (`walkMap` in `ai.ts`, Nov 12): the unit's water cost and jump count; the straight line left units standing on neighboring rooftops. A half-tile token walks it as its square, with the same moves as `reachable` (`squareMoves`, Nov 14 review: a 1-cell walker took gaps the token cannot).
- **`battle/fft/` is a reference study, not the game** (Nov 11): Final Fantasy Tactics formulas and data sourced in `docs/game-design/fft-reference.md`. It only exists for whoever calls `registerFftStudy()` (client with `?scenario=fft-gariland` or `?scenario=fft-classes`, simulator, tests). A rule only the study needs goes in as an optional field in `rules.ts`; a game scenario does not use `rules`. Facing (`UnitState.facing`) is on every map since Nov 13 (units spawn facing the nearest enemy; an older save is filled in), and counter-attacks (`counters` in `combat.ts`) work everywhere; only the counters of `UnitState.vars` (from `UnitDef.vars`) exist for scenarios that ask for them.
