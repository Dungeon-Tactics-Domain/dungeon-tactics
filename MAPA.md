# MAPA — what each file does

Generated by reading the code on Nov 16, 2026; update when a file is added, moved or split (`tests/map.test.ts` fails
when a `.ts` file of `src/` is missing here). One line per file; the rules of each folder are in its `CLAUDE.md`, the
reasons behind them in `docs/decisions.md`.

## src/shared/battle — battle rules (no DOM, no Three.js)

- `src/shared/battle/types.ts` — battle state types (BattleState, UnitState, TurnState, Charge, BattleEvent), plain JSON data.
- `src/shared/battle/units.ts` — UnitDef type, elements, resistances and dodge, the UNIT_DEFS registry built from content, baseDef and isObjective.
- `src/shared/battle/abilities.ts` — AbilityDef type, the ABILITIES registry built from content and a charge's defaultLock.
- `src/shared/battle/equipment.ts` — ItemDef type, slots, the ITEMS registry and bonusOf (a loadout's stats summed on a sheet).
- `src/shared/battle/scenarios.ts` — Scenario (map plus spawns), the MAPS and SCENARIOS registries, FIRST_BATTLE and initialState.
- `src/shared/battle/grid.ts` — tiles with terrain, height, water depth and ramps; parseGrid and withRamps build maps; standing, step cost, facing and sides.
- `src/shared/battle/pathing.ts` — per-class jump, footprints of 2x2 tokens, leaps, reachable and pathTo, where nobody stops (noStopAt: map bodies and corpses), ranges in cells and areas.
- `src/shared/battle/shot.ts` — the shot path (`traceShot`, `shotAt`): what stops a shot and the two ends it is measured between.
- `src/shared/battle/combat.ts` — hit chance, damage range, preview, resolve (hit then damage), the counters it plays, spikes and the battle outcome.
- `src/shared/battle/counter.ts` — who answers a strike with a counter and which counters a move owes.
- `src/shared/battle/push.ts` — where a push ends and what it costs (bump, fall), without touching the state.
- `src/shared/battle/turn-queue.ts` — FFT CT queue: turn cost, the next turn, charges firing and previewOrder for the turn list.
- `src/shared/battle/battle.ts` — Battle: owns the state, move/act/end turn, limited undo (dice never roll back), save/load through parseBattleState.
- `src/shared/battle/rng.ts` — seeded mulberry32 whose state lives in BattleState (nextRng, rollInt).
- `src/shared/battle/rules.ts` — calculations a scenario may swap (survival, crawl rooms, the developer battle) and rulesOf.
- `src/shared/battle/half.ts` — half tiles: halve splits a map into 2x2 cells and units into 2x2 tokens (halfDef, SUB, the ?scenario=half test map).
- `src/shared/battle/ai.ts` — greedy one-turn AI with fixed weights: walkMap, chooseTurn, chooseRetreat, playAiTurn.
- `src/shared/battle/simulate.ts` — AI against AI until the end or a turn cap, for the simulator and tests.

## src/shared/crawl — dungeon crawl rules

- `src/shared/crawl/generate.ts` — seeded room generator from the content tables (generateRoom, roomGroup, roomId, CRAWL_VERSION).
- `src/shared/crawl/verify.ts` — checks a room on the halved map with the real reachable and sight; returns its problems in words.
- `src/shared/crawl/expedition.ts` — floors of rooms from a seed, the life carried between rooms (winRoom) and parseExpedition for dt:crawl.

## src/content — game content as typed data

Each map in `maps/` is a Scenario registered in MAPS and SCENARIOS (src/shared/battle/scenarios.ts), its tiles built by
parseGrid and withRamps (grid.ts).

- `src/content/units.ts` — classes and enemies (UNIT_LIST); type and registry in src/shared/battle/units.ts.
- `src/content/abilities.ts` — abilities (ABILITY_LIST) and the A() builder with the defaults; type and registry in src/shared/battle/abilities.ts.
- `src/content/items.ts` — equipment (ITEM_LIST); type, registry and bonusOf in src/shared/battle/equipment.ts.
- `src/content/party.ts` — party classes (JOBS), the 4 story members and the recruit names in order; rules in src/client/group.ts.
- `src/content/faces.json` — painted face sheets for the dialogue box and the battle HUD.
- `src/content/crawl/rooms.ts` — crawl party, enemy groups by depth, the boss group, room themes, the expedition numbers (CRAWL_RUN) and the gold drops (CRAWL_GOLD); read by generateRoom (src/shared/crawl/generate.ts) and expeditionMap and winRoom (expedition.ts).
- `src/content/crawl/town.ts` — the walkable town map (TOWN), its places and doors, the start, the Guild spots and the townsfolk with their rumours; rules in src/client/town-map.ts, walking in src/client/game/town.ts.
- `src/content/crawl/residents.ts` — the residents of Vauclair and their lines by sentence milestone or after a fall (RESIDENTS, OTHER_PRISONERS); data only, no interface reads it yet
- `src/content/maps/beacon-hill.ts` — Beacon Hill, the 20x20 first battle and standalone default, with its per-tile looks.
- `src/content/maps/skirmish.ts` — 8x8 Skirmish map for tests and quick simulation.
- `src/content/maps/flooded-crypt.ts` — Flooded Crypt, an enclosed 14x14 dungeon, and the CRYPT_KEY letter table.
- `src/content/maps/crypt-heart.ts` — Crypt Heart, 12x12 with a boss altar behind a deep moat.
- `src/content/maps/ash-wood.ts` — Ash Wood, 12x12 cut by a deep river with a bridge and a ford.
- `src/content/maps/frost-pass.ts` — Frost Pass, a 12x14 climb between cliffs to the enemies' summit.

## src/client — wiring and windows

- `src/client/entry.ts` — the entry point (index.html): opens the town or builds the battle the address asks for, its scene and its Game, behind the loading cover until everything landed; a change of screen lets the one in view go and runs the same path on the new address, in the same page; the start banner (announce).
- `src/client/nav.ts` — the one page's screens: `navigate` (to the screen an address opens, with no reload; the address replaced, never pushed) and what leaves with a screen (`screenSignal`, `onLeave`, `leaveScreen`); no imports.
- `src/client/ready-gate.ts` — the readiness gate (`whenReady`): the interface is built only once the models, the 3D pieces' chunks and the fonts landed, and the cover leaves after it drew; the loading bar's share, how long the cover waits for files and the 3D loading screen's layout; pure.
- `src/client/loading.ts` — the loading cover over the whole page (`loadingCover`): "Loading the game" over a bar filled by the files landed, and a Reload button when the load fails, drawn in 3D over the unseen HTML ones; holds the hosted fonts too.
- `src/client/no3d.ts` — no3d(): the warning that replaces the page when there is no WebGL or its context is lost (the interface is only 3D).
- `src/client/main.ts` — Game: wires rules, scene, HUD and input for one battle and owns the turn loop.
- `src/client/hero-turn.ts` — HeroTurn: the hero's turn screens (menu, submenu, map, strike preview "Execute?", lock, facing); owns the screen, the Ui and the cursor and turns keys, clicks and taps into the next screen.
- `src/client/battle-input.ts` — battle input: keyboard, mouse or touch on the canvas and the gamepad, sent to the Game as the same actions.
- `src/client/fps.ts` — the performance counter in the corner of the battle (F3, dt:fps).
- `src/client/before-event.ts` — what plays before each battle event: its sound, the enemy's destination and the ability banner.
- `src/client/town-ui.ts` — what the town's windows share: the saved party's load and save, the gold counter and the reasons a button is off.
- `src/client/town-shop.ts` — openShop() and openBar(): the Shop and the Tavern, handed to the 3D town (town3d/screens.ts); what an item and a recruit say.
- `src/client/town-party.ts` — openParty(): the Party screen (FFT's formation and unit menus), handed to the 3D town (town3d/screens.ts) with the member and page to open on.
- `src/client/sheet-lines.ts` — the numbers a sheet and a piece show: one line per stat, with where each total comes from, its bar and what a piece would change.
- `src/client/town-map.ts` — walkable town rules: the halved map, who stands where, places and doors, routes and strolls; dt:town-grid; the walk's circle, stick and target.
- `src/client/town-pad.ts` — the town's touch pad: the saved side of the joystick (dt:town-pad), the stick's vector from the finger; pure. Drawn by town3d/pad3d.ts, its unseen skeleton in game/town-hud.ts.
- `src/client/dev-roster.ts` — developer cast rules: build and validate sheets, DEV_MAPS, parseSetup and buildDevScenario.
- `src/client/survival.ts` — "Until dawn" standalone mode: its scenario, turn goal and best-record save.

## src/client — pure interface logic (no DOM, no Three.js)

- `src/client/menu-state.ts` — whether the command menu and "Execute?" show (menuUp) and what a tap on an empty tile, the hero, its sheet, another unit or a turn card does to it; pure.
- `src/client/unit-peek.ts` — another unit's look-only mini menu (Status, Abilities, Show range) in the notice window.
- `src/client/command.ts` — FFT command menu (Move, Abilities, Wait, Status): which turn screen shows, what each key leads to and where Cancel goes back to.
- `src/client/interaction.ts` — what a tap or confirm does in battle and the highlights, aim mark and threat the scene draws.
- `src/client/corpse-squares.ts` — groups a resumed battle's corpse cells into one body per token square (corpseSquares).
- `src/client/charge-lines.ts` — charge and counter lines and notes computed from the rule that fires them.
- `src/client/turn-marks.ts` — what the scene marks on the hero's turn: tiles, cursor frame, range ring, shot line, charge preview and counters (turnMarks).
- `src/client/turn-list.ts` — rows of the turn list: the queue projection while aiming (queueRows), round length, two rounds and scrolling, and each unit's number on the map (turnOrder).
- `src/client/battle-text.ts` — battle texts: ability sheet, last action summary (eventLog), attribute rows and the Status sheet.
- `src/client/tile-info.ts` — the tile line under the cursor: X, Y and Z (height), floor, water, spikes and a charge landing there.
- `src/client/forecast.ts` — strike preview per target (hit, damage or heal, push, life after) and its texts.
- `src/client/place-beside.ts` — where a menu goes beside the acting unit on screen (placeBeside) and where the help line above it ends (helpEnd); pure.
- `src/client/wrap-lines.ts` — wrapLines: a text broken into the lines that fit a width, given a measurer; pure, shared by the 3D text and the HUD's sizes.
- `src/client/battle-face.ts` — which face sheet and expression a battle event shows, and for how long.
- `src/client/motion.ts` — the Reduce motion option (dt:motion: System, Yes, No): `reducedMotion()` for the code and `data-motion` on <html> for the CSS.
- `src/client/hit-weight.ts` — weight of a blow: its distances, the number's size and colour, the killing-blow lookahead and the death slow motion option (dt:death-slowmo).
- `src/client/tally.ts` — damage and takedowns per unit from the played events and the heroes' cards (endHeroes), for the result board.
- `src/client/expedition-places.ts` — the expedition map as a chart (pure): each place's spot and state, the ways between them, the floors' rows and a place's gold range.
- `src/client/group.ts` — party rules: recruit, dismiss, class change, who goes to battle and the list's order (dt:party).
- `src/client/chest.ts` — gold, items and per-member equipment (dt:chest, dt:equip) with their parsers.
- `src/client/saves.ts` — save slots: the dt:saves manifest, new game, load and delete, slot summaries.
- `src/client/store.ts` — dt:* storage with a memory fallback, the key MIGRATIONS and the URL param reader.
- `src/client/gesture.ts` — two fingers on the map: the twist that turns the camera an eighth (twist, angleOf); pure.
- `src/client/keyboard.ts` — key to action (keyAction), grid steps by screen direction and the help list.
- `src/client/gamepad.ts` — gamepad button state to keyboard actions.
- `src/client/glyphs.ts` — the key hints' one table (action, key, pad button), the controller's family by its id (familyOf) and what the hints show by the Options and the input last used (shownFor, capsOf, hintLine); pure.
- `src/client/glyph-art.ts` — a key cap or a pad button drawn on a canvas in the game's style (capCanvas), from the proportions and colours of glyph-art.json.
- `src/client/key-hints.ts` — the key hints' live state (dt:glyphs, the input last used, padSeen) and the HTML hint that redraws itself (hint, live, liveText); the Tutorial (dt:tutorial) and Key and button hints (dt:hints) options.
- `src/client/markers.ts` — unit heights and marker heights above them (ring, bar, cursor) and the model of each sheet.
- `src/client/water-levels.ts` — water surface and wading heights for drawing.
- `src/client/away.ts` — the game left in the background is paused: the first frame back drops the time away (awayFactor).
- `src/client/timing.ts` — turn-flow timings in one place and the speed-up.
- `src/client/i18n.ts` — game language, t('pt', 'en'), unitName and abilityName.
