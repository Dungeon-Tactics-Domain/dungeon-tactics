# MAPA — what each file does

Generated by reading the code on Nov 16, 2026; update when a file is added, moved or split (`tests/map.test.ts` fails
when a `.ts` file of `src/` is missing here). One line per file; the rules of each folder are in its `CLAUDE.md`, the
reasons behind them in `docs/decisions.md`.

## src/shared/battle — battle rules (no DOM, no Three.js)

- `src/shared/battle/types.ts` — battle state types (BattleState, UnitState, TurnState, Charge, BattleEvent), plain JSON data.
- `src/shared/battle/units.ts` — UnitDef type, elements, resistances and dodge, the UNIT_DEFS registry built from content, baseDef and isObjective.
- `src/shared/battle/abilities.ts` — AbilityDef type, the ABILITIES registry built from content and a charge's defaultLock.
- `src/shared/battle/equipment.ts` — ItemDef type, slots, the ITEMS registry and bonusOf (a loadout's stats summed on a sheet).
- `src/shared/battle/scenarios.ts` — Scenario (map plus spawns), the MAPS and SCENARIOS registries, FIRST_BATTLE and initialState.
- `src/shared/battle/grid.ts` — tiles with terrain, height, water depth and ramps; parseGrid and withRamps build maps; standing, step cost, facing and sides.
- `src/shared/battle/pathing.ts` — per-class jump, footprints of 2x2 tokens, leaps, reachable and pathTo, ranges in cells, areas, line of sight and the shot path.
- `src/shared/battle/combat.ts` — hit chance, damage range, preview, resolve (hit then damage), counters, push and falls, spikes and the battle outcome.
- `src/shared/battle/turn-queue.ts` — FFT CT queue: turn cost, the next turn, charges firing and previewOrder for the turn list.
- `src/shared/battle/battle.ts` — Battle: owns the state, move/act/end turn, limited undo (dice never roll back), save/load through parseBattleState.
- `src/shared/battle/rng.ts` — seeded mulberry32 whose state lives in BattleState (nextRng, rollInt).
- `src/shared/battle/rules.ts` — calculations a scenario may swap (only the FFT study does) and rulesOf.
- `src/shared/battle/half.ts` — half tiles: halve splits a map into 2x2 cells and units into 2x2 tokens (halfDef, SUB, the ?scenario=half test map).
- `src/shared/battle/ai.ts` — greedy one-turn AI with fixed weights: walkMap, chooseTurn, chooseRetreat, playAiTurn.
- `src/shared/battle/simulate.ts` — AI against AI until the end or a turn cap, for the simulator and tests.
- `src/shared/battle/fft/data.ts` — Final Fantasy Tactics reference data (classes, weapons, gear, skills) for the balance study.
- `src/shared/battle/fft/formulas.ts` — Final Fantasy Tactics reference formulas (damage, hit, crit, fall) for the balance study.
- `src/shared/battle/fft/gariland.ts` — FFT study scenarios on our engine (Gariland, the classes battle) and registerFftStudy.

## src/shared/crawl — dungeon crawl rules

- `src/shared/crawl/generate.ts` — seeded room generator from the content tables (generateRoom, roomGroup, roomId, CRAWL_VERSION).
- `src/shared/crawl/verify.ts` — checks a room on the halved map with the real reachable and sight; returns its problems in words.
- `src/shared/crawl/expedition.ts` — floors of rooms from a seed, the life carried between rooms (winRoom) and parseExpedition for dt:crawl.

## src/content — game content as typed data

Each map in `maps/` is a Scenario registered in MAPS and SCENARIOS (src/shared/battle/scenarios.ts), its tiles built by
parseGrid and withRamps (grid.ts).

- `src/content/units.ts` — classes and enemies (UNIT_LIST); type and registry in src/shared/battle/units.ts.
- `src/content/abilities.ts` — abilities (ABILITY_LIST) and the A() builder with the defaults; type and registry in src/shared/battle/abilities.ts.
- `src/content/items.ts` — equipment (ITEM_LIST); type, registry and bonusOf in src/shared/battle/equipment.ts.
- `src/content/party.ts` — party classes (JOBS), the 4 story members and the recruit names in order; rules in src/client/group.ts.
- `src/content/faces.json` — painted face sheets for the dialogue box and the battle HUD.
- `src/content/crawl/rooms.ts` — crawl party, enemy groups by depth, the boss group, room themes and the expedition numbers (CRAWL_RUN); read by generateRoom (src/shared/crawl/generate.ts) and expeditionMap and winRoom (expedition.ts).
- `src/content/crawl/town.ts` — the walkable town map (TOWN), its places and doors, the start, the Guild spots and the townsfolk with their rumours; rules in src/client/town-map.ts, walking in src/client/game/town.ts.
- `src/content/maps/beacon-hill.ts` — Beacon Hill, the 20x20 first battle and standalone default, with its per-tile looks.
- `src/content/maps/skirmish.ts` — 8x8 Skirmish map for tests and quick simulation.
- `src/content/maps/flooded-crypt.ts` — Flooded Crypt, an enclosed 14x14 dungeon, and the CRYPT_KEY letter table.
- `src/content/maps/crypt-heart.ts` — Crypt Heart, 12x12 with a boss altar behind a deep moat.
- `src/content/maps/ash-wood.ts` — Ash Wood, 12x12 cut by a deep river with a bridge and a ford.
- `src/content/maps/frost-pass.ts` — Frost Pass, a 12x14 climb between cliffs to the enemies' summit.

## src/client — wiring and windows

- `src/client/main.ts` — Game: the entry point; wires rules, scene, HUD and input and owns the turn loop.
- `src/client/town-ui.ts` — the town's windows: Party with each member's sheet, Tavern (recruits) and Shop.
- `src/client/town-map.ts` — walkable town rules: the halved map, who stands where, places and doors, routes and strolls; dt:town-grid.
- `src/client/dev-menu.ts` — developer menu screen (map, cast, JSON import/export); rules in dev-roster.ts.
- `src/client/dev-roster.ts` — developer cast rules: build and validate sheets, DEV_MAPS, parseSetup and buildDevScenario.
- `src/client/survival.ts` — "Until dawn" standalone mode: its scenario, turn goal and best-record save.

## src/client — pure interface logic (no DOM, no Three.js)

- `src/client/command.ts` — FFT command menu (Move, Act, Wait, Status): which turn screen shows and what each key leads to.
- `src/client/interaction.ts` — what a tap or confirm does in battle and the highlights, aim mark and threat the scene draws.
- `src/client/charge-lines.ts` — charge and counter lines and notes computed from the rule that fires them.
- `src/client/turn-list.ts` — rows of the turn list: the queue projection while aiming (queueRows), round length, two rounds and scrolling.
- `src/client/battle-text.ts` — battle texts: ability sheet, last action summary (eventLog), attribute rows and the Status sheet.
- `src/client/tile-info.ts` — the tile line under the cursor: height, floor, water, spikes and a charge landing there.
- `src/client/forecast.ts` — strike preview per target (hit, damage or heal, push, life after) and its texts.
- `src/client/place-beside.ts` — where a menu goes beside the acting unit on screen (placeBeside).
- `src/client/battle-face.ts` — which face sheet and expression a battle event shows, and for how long.
- `src/client/tally.ts` — damage and takedowns per unit from the played events, for the result board.
- `src/client/group.ts` — party rules: recruit, dismiss, class change, who goes to battle (dt:party).
- `src/client/chest.ts` — gold, items and per-member equipment (dt:chest, dt:equip) with their parsers.
- `src/client/saves.ts` — save slots: the dt:saves manifest, new game, load and delete, slot summaries.
- `src/client/store.ts` — dt:* storage with a memory fallback, the key MIGRATIONS and the URL param reader.
- `src/client/keyboard.ts` — key to action (keyAction), grid steps by screen direction and the help list.
- `src/client/gamepad.ts` — gamepad button state to keyboard actions.
- `src/client/pixel-settings.ts` — pixel art options (dt:pixel) and their parser.
- `src/client/markers.ts` — unit heights and marker heights above them (ring, bar, cursor) and the model of each sheet.
- `src/client/water-levels.ts` — water surface and wading heights for drawing.
- `src/client/timing.ts` — turn-flow timings in one place and the speed-up.
- `src/client/i18n.ts` — game language, t('pt', 'en'), unitName and abilityName.

## src/client — DOM interface

- `src/client/theme.ts` — interface theme choice (dt:theme), set as data-theme on the page.
- `src/client/hud.ts` — BattleHud: the battle HUD in the FFT layout (tile line, turn list, menus, strike strips, unit panels, help line), built from the modules beside it.
- `src/client/dialog.ts` — dialog(), choice(), slider() and the window rows; focus, Esc and inert background.
- `src/client/icons.ts` — el(), button(), iconButton() and icon() over the icon sprite, and the ability icon atlas.
- `src/client/talk.ts` — dialogue box: typed text, blips, talking faces and text speed (dt:text).
- `src/client/touch-input.ts` — tap, drag and pinch or wheel on the map.
- `src/client/style.css` — theme tokens (:root[data-theme]) and every component's style.

## src/client — 3D drawing (Three.js) and sound

- `src/client/scene.ts` — BattleScene: renderer with the pixel pass, lights, frame loop and the scene's public API.
- `src/client/three-util.ts` — small shared Three helpers: seeded draws, noise and KayKit palette textures without mipmaps.
- `src/client/terrain.ts` — smooth painted terrain mesh, its extension ring and the river that continues off the map.
- `src/client/water.ts` — water surface with the wave field (WaveField) the units disturb.
- `src/client/trees.ts` — painted trees built in code that replace the KayKit tree models.
- `src/client/props.ts` — painted crates, barrels, rocks and litter built in code that replace KayKit props.
- `src/client/painted.ts` — the shared painted material and the shape helpers trees and props are built from.
- `src/client/bridge.ts` — bridges with planks, beams, posts, ropes and end stairs.
- `src/client/detail.ts` — grain texture over the remaining KayKit pieces.
- `src/client/ambience.ts` — Ambience: the surroundings beyond the board edge (ground, rings of scenery), built per mood and lit by the phase.
- `src/client/ambience-moods.ts` — the surroundings' moods (forest, field, crypt, frost, cave), the map each goes with and the URL switches.
- `src/client/ambience-effects.ts` — AirEffects: torch and crystal glows, low mist, light rays and particles.
- `src/client/ambience-crypt.ts` — the crypt surroundings: walls, torches, lit corner posts and crystals.
- `src/client/ambience-fog.ts` — the surroundings' fog shader pieces, its material and the skip for the pixel normal pass.
- `src/client/fx.ts` — gesture clip and effect per ability (playFx on impact, chargeAura while charging).
- `src/client/sound.ts` — music playback, effects, fanfares and blips in WebAudio, and sound settings (dt:sound).
- `src/client/music.ts` — track list, each track's file and gain.

## src/client/game — what opens around the battle

- `src/client/game/boot.ts` — reads the URL and picks the battle to open (expedition room, crawl room, study, developer, map, standalone).
- `src/client/game/title.ts` — title screen and its windows (new game, continue, battle, how to play, credits, developer).
- `src/client/game/town.ts` — the walkable town: drawn by the battle scene, walked by the first deployed hero, talking and doors on the action button.
- `src/client/game/crawl.ts` — the town menu and quest gate, the expedition map, room settle and finish, and the way home.
- `src/client/game/saves-ui.ts` — Continue's list of save slots (load, delete) and starting a new game.
- `src/client/game/options.ts` — Options window (game, sound, video, controls, language; restart, home, developer) and the town grid switch.
- `src/client/game/end-screens.ts` — end-of-battle window, the result board and the victory confetti.

## src/client/scene — the parts BattleScene composes

- `src/client/scene/board.ts` — Board: block columns, painted terrain, water, bridges, walls, trees and details built once from the map.
- `src/client/scene/board-looks.ts` — what each tile look draws (LOOK pieces, side colors, details) and the pieces a map downloads.
- `src/client/scene/board-extras.ts` — board dressing outside the rules: edge sides, caravan bodies, dotted border, crypt stairs, rocks and tufts.
- `src/client/scene/tree-crowns.ts` — TreeCrowns: forest-tile trees and the crown cutout over units, cursor and marks.
- `src/client/scene/units.ts` — Units: character models, rings, facing arrows, health bars and the event animations on them.
- `src/client/scene/marks.ts` — Marks: tile marks, charge danger, path dots, cursor, range ring and shot trajectory.
- `src/client/scene/charge-lines.ts` — ChargeLines: draws the charge and counter lines on top of everything.
- `src/client/scene/camera.ts` — CameraRig: orthographic camera, zoom, 45° rotation, follow, pan, title spin and action camera.
- `src/client/scene/space.ts` — TileSpace: tile to world coordinates on slopes and water, ramps and squareUnder.
- `src/client/scene/timeline.ts` — Timeline: the battle clock behind every animate and wait (no setTimeout).

## src/client/dev — localhost only

- `src/client/dev/sandbox-spec.ts` — sandbox scene spec: parsing, checks and steps, shared with scripts/sandbox.mjs.
- `src/client/dev/sandbox.ts` — stages a battle from a spec and exposes window.__sandbox.
- `src/client/dev/arena.ts` — the animation arena map for the sandbox bench (?scenario=arena).

## Other folders

- `scripts/` — battle-sim.ts (optional AI vs AI simulator), crawl-sheet.ts (crawl room screenshots), smoke.mjs and smoke-game.mjs (headless checks), sandbox.mjs and sandbox/ (scene specs and clips), portraits.mjs, slim-character.mjs, lib/ (browser launcher), assets/ (icon, texture, face and model generators), music/ (track generators).
- `tests/` — Vitest: one `<area>-<topic>.test.ts` per rule or pure client module, battle-helpers.ts for shared setup, setup.ts for the environment; architecture.test.ts and map.test.ts check the source tree itself.
- `docs/` — game-design/README.md (index and what each lab touches), design spec, pillars, party and towns design, FFT/Tactics Ogre references, design system, decisions log, assets index.
- `assets/` — source packs (KayKit, sounds); not shipped.
