# Client: battle screen, windows and input

What the battle interface does and why, moved out of `src/client/CLAUDE.md` (the rules of code stay there). The [design system](design-system.md) is the source of truth for tokens and pieces; this file holds the behaviour of each screen and the request behind it. Decisions with their dates are also in [docs/decisions.md](../decisions.md).

## FFT battle-screen style

Fernando's decision, Nov 12: "discard the current interfaces and recreate the interface the same way as the original final fantasy tactics". The list below says what is implemented.

- **Turn screens** (`Screen` in `command.ts`): command menu → abilities submenu → map (choose the tile) → "Execute?" (the strike preview) → menu. Waiting leads to the direction choice on maps with direction.
  - Names, order and steps follow FFT: The Ivalice Chronicles (Fernando, Nov 16; captures in the labs' `refs/fft-ivalice`): Move · Abilities · Wait · Status (the row was "Act"; its id in code is still `act`), with "Undo move" under Move after a move (Nov 18; `menuRows`, so four rows at the turn's start and up to five). The reference's fifth row, Auto-battle, is left out: the game has no auto play for the player. Its Items and job-command lists inside Abilities are left out too: a unit's abilities are one flat list.
  - Move walks on confirming the tile (the reference's "Move Here"). An ability's target opens its preview, where the action's verb (Attack, Shoot, Cast, Heal or Use; Nov 18) runs it and Cancel goes back to aiming with the target still marked; the preview has no Yes/No to toggle (arrows do nothing there; on the lock step they switch the lock).
  - Cancel (Esc, Backspace, B, right button, the Back button) goes back one step, to the row it came from (`cancelStep` in `command.ts`, pure): lock step → preview → Act map → submenu on the same ability → menu on Abilities; a self-centred ability's preview → submenu; Move map → menu on Move; facing → menu on Wait; on the menu, it undoes a free move, else nothing.
- **What is not copied:** art, font, icons, portraits, frames, sounds and text of the original. Panels are drawn in 3D from our own tokens (the `--ui3d-*` tokens at the top of `style.css`, see the design system), portraits are our KayKit characters (`public/chars`, `scripts/portraits.mjs`), and our own ▶ arrow takes the place of the menu hand.

## BattleHud pieces

- **Top left corner:** the tile under the cursor (`tileInfo`) and the list of upcoming turns.
  - Since Nov 15 (Fernando's decision; the short list of 4 and its L key are gone) every unit of the battle in compact rows: 36 px face beside the number or NOW and the name with ● or ◆; a queued charge is its own row with the caster's face, the ability icon and "resolves in N" ticks.
  - Two rounds, each row with a small round mark 1 or 2. While aiming (Act map and "Execute?") it is a copy of the queue where the one acting pays the cost of acting (100 after moving, 80 without) and a charged ability's row sits where it fires. Pure parts in `turn-list.ts`; the projection is `previewOrder` with its `now` argument.
  - It scrolls within the column above the acting panel (wheel, PgUp/PgDn, right stick): a new turn scrolls back to NOW, a redraw keeps where the player scrolled, and the charge row of "Execute?" is kept in view.
- **Turn order on the map** (Nov 17, Fernando: "the turn order as a number near each character's health bar"): every
  unit carries the number of its next turn's row in the list (`turnOrder` in `turn-list.ts`, from the rows the list
  itself shows, so it follows the copy of the queue while aiming), glued to the left of its health bar (to its right while the
  turn list would cover it: `sideOrder`, every frame) and facing the
  camera with it (`setOrder` in `scene/unit-view.ts`). Big digits (`MARKER.orderH`, about 32 px at the whole-map zoom of
  a 1080p page) in the side's colour, blue for heroes and pink for enemies, gold for the one acting now; always on, for
  both sides. Beside the bar, so it clears the acting unit's arrow and stays above the head of the Brute and the
  Colossus (`tests/mark-style.test.ts`). A unit that never plays (the Beacon) has none; the town has none.
- **Painted faces** (Nov 15, Fernando's request): the unit panels and the turn list show the dialogue box's sheet (`faceSheet` in `talk.ts`) instead of the portrait, with an expression from the events `Game.animate` plays.
  - Pure rules in `battle-face.ts` (sheet by class, `faceCues`, `expressionAt`, tested); moods kept by `Game` on the battle clock (`BattleScene.now`); frames moved every frame by `BattleHud.showFaces` without a redraw.
  - The end-of-battle board shows a hero row per hero who fought, the fallen included (`resultBoard` in `game/end-screens.ts`, heroes from `endHeroes` in `tally.ts`: the roster, since the state drops the fallen), with a still face (`stillFace` in `hud-pieces.ts`) by `endExpression`.
  - Outside the battle the Party screen's list and Status head (`town-party.ts`) and the Tavern's recruit rows (`town-shop.ts`) show the class's neutral still face. Units without a sheet keep `portrait()`.
- **Mana and stamina** (Nov 16; the rules in `src/shared/CLAUDE.md`): the unit panels add an MP (mana, `--mp`) or ST (stamina, `--sp`) bar for the pool the sheet has (`poolGauges`), the Status window its left/max and regen (`poolRows`), the party sheet the full bars and the regen line (`regenLine`). Life regen from the pieces shows on Status beside the life ("210/210, +10 HP per turn") and in the same regen line; when it comes back at the start of a turn the unit floats a green number like a heal and the log says it as its own sentence ("Warrior +10 regen", `regen` event).
  - The abilities submenu shows the cost and the charge ticks; a row it cannot pay is grayed out and says how much is missing (`shortOf`); "Execute?" says what it spends (`spendNote`); the expedition map lists each hero's mana.
  - `newBattle` (`game/boot.ts`) sets the carried mana and gives the first actor the regen `Battle.start` spent on the full pool.
- **Top middle:** the help line and, below it, the ability name banner.
- **Beside the acting character** (Nov 13, Fernando's request: "beside the character like in FFT"): the Move · (Undo move) · Abilities · Wait · Status menu, the submenu and the "Execute?" window with hit chance, effect and life before → after.
  - Since Nov 15 the submenu is at the lab kit's size: a head with the menu name, 60 px rows with the 32 px ability icon or a glass orb before the name, Act marked as picked while the submenu is open.
  - `BattleScene.screenOf` gives the character on screen; `placeBeside` (`place-beside.ts`, pure and tested) picks the side (right; with no room, left) and slides in height so as not to cover the turn list or the "Execute?" target (with no room clear of both, "Execute?" keeps only the turn list clear).
  - `Game.placeMenu` repositions every frame (only left/top, without redoing the DOM), because the camera slides and rotates with the menu open.
- **Undo the move** (Nov 13, Fernando's request, like Tactics Ogre; `canUndoMove` and `menuRows` in `command.ts`): after a free move an "Undo move" row shows right under Move and Esc (B, right button) on the menu undoes the move for free. Since Nov 18 (Fernando) it is a row of its own: Move stays while movement is left (it opens the range that remains) and leaves once none is; at the turn's start the menu has four rows, after a move with movement left five (Move, Undo move, Abilities, Wait, Status), and the menu grows upwards from its foot. After acting, Esc undoes a free move (one that drew no counter) and Z undoes the rest; Esc, P and Z do not repeat when held, and the menu cursor never starts on "Undo move" (on Move while movement is left, otherwise on Abilities or Wait).
- **Charges** (Nov 15, Fernando): confirming a charge aimed at a unit adds a lock step in the same "Execute?" window (On the character / On the tile, starting on the ability's default lock; the two sit side by side, so left and right go to the one on that side and stay there, up and down do nothing (`lockStep`, Nov 17); Back goes back to "Execute?"; `needsLock` in `command.ts`), and its note says when it goes off and the movement left after starting it.
  - Every charging unit, enemies included, keeps a line to what it is locked on. On the charging unit's Move screen the line is traced again from the tile under the cursor, with the help line naming who it would hit.
  - `charge-lines.ts` (pure and tested, from the rule that fires the charge) computes the lines; `scene/charge-lines.ts` draws them over everything, along the shot's own path (a cast spell's as a straight line). Past the point where the shot stops the line goes red with an X, orange on the caster's own side (friendly fire, half).
- **Shot preview and flight** (the trajectory grill of Nov 15, from the Mechanics lab's prototype): aiming a flying ability (`needsSight`) on the Act map and "Execute?" shows the rule's own path to the cursor (`traceShot` in `turn-marks.ts`, drawn by `Marks.setTrajectory` with `TileSpace.point`): dots flowing from the shooter's 1.5 m point to the same point over the target, a landing ring on its floor, and past where the shot stops red dots and a red X facing the camera.
  - A spell cast on the target (Blast, Heal) shows a circle pulsing over its area (red for damage, green for a heal) and a still dashed line from the caster, with no dots and no X. The range marks turn to the blocked cross from the same path (`interaction.ts`).
  - The `attack` event carries the `Shot`, and `fx.ts` `fly` follows it up to the stop (`scene/unit-events.ts`), the tip on the path's tangent, a short trail and a spark where it lands, at the lab's speeds (the bow's curve slower than a straight throw or bolt); the struck unit's shake is the usual hit's. An attack without a path keeps the old lob.
- **Counters** (Nov 15, Fernando; redefined the same day: only in the turn of the strike, on walking out of the struck unit's reach or, for a shooter struck up close, out of its contact):
  - Aiming: each target that could strike the actor where it stands, or a shooter it stands too close to, shows it (`countersOwed`: a ranged basic attack as its shot's line back to the attacker, a melee one as a violet arrow over its head pointing at it), named in "Execute?".
  - On the Move screen after a strike, the stops that draw a counter (out of a reach, or out of a shooter's contact and in its range, its line then ending at the stop) get a red frame (`counter` in `highlights`). On such a stop: the counter's line or arrow (`countersFrom`, from `pendingCounters`), the move help, and a note on the cursor with the Strike icon and the damage at half (`counterDamage`, `BattleScene.setCursorNote`).
- **Bottom:** panel of the acting unit (left) and of the unit under the cursor (right), with portrait and HP, MP and CT bars. Since Nov 15 at the lab kit's size (`--slot-w`); below 1600 px wide the portrait and the command menu shrink, because at the kit's size they covered the unit at 1280. The target panel is mirrored (portrait on the right).
- **Strike preview** (Nov 13, Fernando's request: "the attack preview interface should be like this one from Tactics Ogre (Reborn)"; our own design; since Nov 15 the lab kit's two-colour strip): when aiming (Act map and "Execute?"), beside the acting unit's panel, in the target panel's place, one strip per target (`strikes`, up to 3, the rest in "+N targets").
  - The action half on the panel blue: ability icon and name, the big damage −12–15 PTS (or +7–10 in green on a heal), ⊕ hit chance. The target half on its side's colour (wine for an enemy): name and side, the life bar with the part that goes away in stripes, and the `strikeInfo` line (↶ from behind / ⇥ from the side, push and spikes, "life 25 → 13–16", ALLY!).
  - One calculation (`forecast` in `forecast.ts`) for the strips and for "Execute?".
  - The strips go into `sizes.bottom` (so "Execute?" does not land on top); one or two stay within the acting panel's height, three rise about 100 px above it at 1920. Only the acting unit's panel counts in the height the scene discounts (`insets`): otherwise the map jumped when aiming.
- **Top right corner** (Fernando, Nov 18 night grill: no ☰ menu, buttons always in sight): the gear opens Options (H); left of it "↶ Desfazer", faded to 45% with no step to undo, and Back (↩), a finger's; the camera pair ↺ ↻ on the top left, right after the turn list. Undo and ↺ ↻ are on by default on touch and off on a PC (Options › Display › Screen buttons; `screen-buttons.ts`). Fast battle, Back to title and the fps counter are rows of Options; Show the whole map keeps only its key (F). The performance counter sits right below.
- **Marks** (Nov 17, Fernando): Options → Video and effects → "Marks": Simple, Light (default), Stone or Neon, saved in `dt:marks` and applied at once (`mark-style.ts`; `?marks=simples|luz|pedra|neon` in the address wins). What each one draws and what it costs: [client-scene.md](client-scene.md), "Mark styles".
- **Interface layout** (Nov 16, Fernando: the places of FFT: The Ivalice Chronicles): Options → Video and effects → "Interface layout", At the screen edges (default) or Centred 16:9 frame, saved in `dt:layout` and applied at once (`layout-mode.ts`; `?layout=edges|frame` in the address wins when the page opens). The places are the regions of `ui3d/layout.ts`. In the frame the HTML panels move in with it (same size); the 3D and CSS pieces below also take its scale. On a 16:9 screen of 1152x648 or more both look the same.
- **Only 3D** (Fernando, Nov 18): every piece is drawn on the shared 3D layer; there is no look flag and no HTML interface (`?ui=`, `?menu=`, `?turn=`, `?menu3d`, `?menucss` and the rest are gone). With no WebGL or a lost context the page shows one plain warning (`no3d.ts`). The client hands each window to its 3D screen through `looks3d.ts` (one slot per screen, filled when the screen is built).
  - Both 3D looks are screens of one WebGL layer over the HUD (`ui3d/layer.ts`), ticked by `BattleHud.showFaces` on the battle clock, drawn only while something moves. A lost context, the layer's or the map's (the scene's canvas, Nov 18), shows the warning of `no3d.ts` in place of the page.
  - The 3D menu takes each turn screen from the HUD (`menu3d/menu-view.ts`: the same rows, names and greyed states as the HTML menu; `changes()` names what to animate), sits where `menu3d/menu-layout.ts` says and brings its own transparent buttons (click a row, mouse over moves the focus, the sheet's foot turns its page). The HTML menu stays unseen for the HUD's own state; only "Execute?" still follows the character.
  - The ability sheet has 3 pages (Description, Effects, Cost: `abilityPages` in `battle-text.ts`, the help line's facts split in three), turned with left and right (`turnPage` in `command.ts`) or its arrows.
  - The banner, the Height and the button hints (`hints3d/`, one screen of the same layer) draw the help line as the reference's pill (an ability's name takes it while it plays), the height of the tile under the cursor in the top right corner (hidden under the abilities submenu) and, at the foot, what Confirm and Cancel do on this screen (`hintsFor`, from `Screen` and `cancelStep`) with the camera and menu buttons, for the device last touched. The help line, the tile line and the HUD's key hints stay in the HTML, unseen; the tile line itself is drawn small under the Height (Nov 16). Hints are one or two words, zoom and rotate as one "Camera" hint, and each help-line instruction fits one pill line (Fernando, Nov 16).
  - `confirm3d/` draws "Execute?" in 3D beside the character, over the unseen HTML window that keeps its place and buttons; with the 3D bar on, the 3D turn list leaves out the acting card (the sheet's portrait is card 1, as on the reference), and the sheet's strip always leads with the class.
  - `window3d/tools3d.ts` draws the corner buttons (gear, Undo, Back, ↺ ↻) over the unseen HTML buttons, which keep their taps; `window3d/status3d.ts` draws the Status window (the C key, the Status command, the ability sheet of the I key) as a parchment sheet in the middle of the darkened battle, over an unseen window of `dialog.ts` (`status-window.ts` hands it the sheet).
  - Both turn lists show each unit's life and CT beside its face (Nov 17, Fernando: "barra de vida com o número e o regen, barra de CT com o número e o ganho"): `unitStats` in `turn-list.ts` gives life, its max and `hpRegen` (life back at each of the unit's turns; only pieces give it), and CT (0 to 100) with the CT gained each tick, which is the sheet's `speed` with the pieces on (the game has no other "CT regen"). The values are the unit's now, repeated on each of its rows: the list projects the order, not the life. The 3D list has no keyboard or gamepad focus of its own, so the long wording (per turn, per tick) shows under the mouse and, for everyone, in the Controls window of the H menu (`game/options-help.ts`); the short lines are always on the card. The 3D card lost the thin life bar under its portrait (the one beside it carries the strike preview's blink), and the 3D bar's sheets show the same two gains (`bar-view.ts`).
  - The 3D turn list reads its cards from the same rows as the HTML list (`turn3d/queue-view.ts`) and takes their boxes from the HUD (`queueBoxes`), so the scroll, PgUp/PgDn and the strike preview's rows work as before; a tip beside the card under the mouse gives its name and life.
  - F3 adds a line with the layer's draw calls and triangles.

## Windows

- **Design system** (Nov 13, Fernando's request): tokens, pieces with their states, input, measured contrast and debts in [design-system.md](design-system.md). Change a token or a piece, change the document in the same commit.
- **One look for everything** (Nov 13, Fernando's choice; FFT Classic since Nov 15): `dialog()` windows on the same blue as the HUD panels: title, Options, Status, How to play, Credits, Party, battle end and Developer. Light text in `--ink`/`--ink-muted`, highlight and link in `--focus` (yellow); since Nov 15 the title on the head band, a bare ✕ and the lab kit's dark blue buttons with the steel trim (the primary in a brighter blue). Lists of places (title, Quick battle, Town) are menu rows on the real focus. Every text colour passes 4.5:1.
- **Title and saved games on their FFT counterparts** (Nov 16, Fernando): the title is the name in the upper part of the art over a small plain command window (no head, no orbs), the cursor on Continue when there is a save; Continue's list reads like a file list, each slot with its number, the leader's face, who goes, where, the gold and when it was last played, in slot order with the cursor on the latest (`game/saves-ui.ts`). The game has no levels and counts no play time, so the list shows neither.
- **Fixed title screen** (Nov 13, Fernando's request: "on the title, pressing Esc starts a battle… I want a real title screen"): `dialog(…, { fixed: true })` does not close; the screens that leave it (Quick battle, How to play, Credits, Developer) go back with Esc/✕ via `onBack`. With any window open, `#app` is `inert` (`dialog.ts`). The Hill in the background is just scenery. From Nov 16 to Nov 18 the title opened before the models (Fernando's grill: it waited ~49 s on 4G for 7.9 MB), in HTML, and changed into the 3D one when the layer arrived. Since Nov 18 (Fernando: "no interfaces swapping out of nowhere") a loading cover (`loading.ts`: a bar of the files landed, drawn in 3D; with no WebGL the warning of `no3d.ts`) stays over the page until the models, the 3D pieces' chunks and the fonts landed and the interface drew (`ready-gate.ts`); the title, the HUD and the town are only built then, in their final look. The expedition map is a fixed dialog over the walkable town (`game/crawl.ts`).
- **Options as one list** (Nov 16, Fernando: the interface on its FFT counterpart; it replaced the wide window with a sections column of Nov 13): `optionsMenu` in `game/options.ts` (opened by `Game.menu`), 860 px wide. The goal line, then one scrolling list of option rows (`option-row.ts`) under the headings Game (text speed, language), Sound and Video and effects, each row with its value at the right between ◀ ▶; left and right change it, Enter goes to the next, and the box under the list says what the row under the cursor does. The list's last rows open the tile marks' legend and the controls in a window over Options (`game/options-help.ts`), which closes back to its row. Restart battle, the way home (Town, or Leave the room in an expedition room) and Developer stay at the foot.
- **Volume as a bar in 5% steps** (Nov 13, Fernando's request): since Nov 16 an option row (`percent()` in `option-row.ts`: a bar and the number in %, applies and saves on every step). Left and right on the keyboard and the gamepad (`padDialog`) step it, a click or tap on the bar sets it. `dt:sound` keeps `{ music, sfx }` from 0 to 100; the old save (0, 1, 2) becomes 0, 45 and 100 in `parseSound`, and 100% is the old "high" (gain 0.8).
- **Pixel art left** (Fernando, Nov 18 night grill, item 11): the game draws smooth 3D only. The pixel pass (`RenderPixelatedPass`, outline, reduced palette, camera locked to the pixel grid), its four Options rows, the V key, the corner menu's Pixel size row, the 3D kit's pixel filter (`?pixel=1`, a spec's `uiPixel`) and `dt:pixel` are gone; a save with pixel art on is no longer read.
- **Performance counter** (Fernando's request, Nov 12): fps, frame time and Three resources (`BattleScene.stats`), plus JS memory when the browser reports it. Off by default since Nov 16 (it cluttered the screen on a tablet); the corner menu row "FPS counter" and F3 toggle it (`dt:fps`), text updated 2 times per second.
- **Developer menu** (Fernando's request, Nov 12): Options (the gear or H) → Developer. Chooses the map (one of `DEV_MAPS` in `dev-roster.ts`: Beacon Hill, Skirmish, Flooded Crypt, Crypt Heart, Ash Wood and Frost Pass), builds heroes and enemies (class, name, life, speed, movement, jump and abilities), saves to `dt:dev`, exports and imports JSON and opens the battle at `?scenario=dev`. Pure rules in `dev-roster.ts` (`buildDevScenario` creates sheets `dev-h0`, `dev-e0`… with the class's character; `parseSetup` is the JSON boundary). Since Nov 18 (Fernando: the whole interface in 3D) the menu opens drawn in 3D (`flow3d/dev3d.ts`): one scrolling parchment form built from the 3D kit's fields (`ui3d/field.ts`), each unit a row that opens its class, name, four numbers, abilities and Remove, the cast also as JSON in a text field (paste and leave it to import); the fields and edits are pure in `flow3d/dev-view.ts`, with the same rules, and a 9th ability is not taken (`parseSetup` refuses a cast with more than 8). Units occupy the map's start tiles; with no Beacon on the map, whoever is left wins.

## End of battle

- **Result board after FFT's results** (Nov 16, Fernando; it was the Design lab's mockup of Nov 15): the standalone battle and the expedition rooms end on `resultBoard` (`game/end-screens.ts`): the title banner, then the party as a list of rows from `endHeroes` in `tally.ts` (roster minus the Beacon; still face, name with the highlight's MVP tag, life bar, damage and takedowns as two number columns) beside the Result window, one line per result (floor, rooms, gold; Until Dawn's turns and record), which ends on the commands. One board, not pages: the banner, the rows and the window come in one after the other and one Enter goes on. The line under a row's life bar says the life carried into the next room in an expedition, or "Fallen" (XP and levels are cancelled). A defeat's commands: "Try again" and the way home in the standalone battle; an expedition's defeat only goes back to town (the expedition is settled before the board opens, so there is nothing to retry). Victory drops confetti (`confetti`). The board is drawn in 3D (`result3d/`, Nov 16), laid out like FFT: The Ivalice Chronicles' victory screen: the title in capitals over a hairline, the note under it, and a dark band across the screen with the heroes' lines and the result's columns on the left and the highlight on the right; the board's own buttons sit unseen over the drawn ones, so Enter, Esc and the pad work as in the window.
- **Battle highlight** (night of Nov 12): `tally.ts` (pure, tested) sums damage and downed units of each unit from the events `Game.animate` receives; the end screen shows the hero who downed the most (tie broken by damage). Memory only: reopening mid-battle resets it.
- **Until Dawn mode** (night of Nov 12): in the Quick battle, `?scenario=survival` assembles in `survival.ts` the 4 heroes against enemies with 2x life that are born on the heroes' side of the river, in Ash Wood (ambush: when born on the other side, just passing the turn won 100%); it wins on reaching `SURVIVE_TURNS` turns (all the turns, the same the list counts: its title shows how many are left) or by downing everyone. Measurement: `MEASURE=300 npx vitest run tests/survival.test.ts --silent=false`.

## Input

Keyboard, mouse and gamepad move the same cursor and go through the same `Game.runKey` (in the walkable town, `Town.run`).

- **Keyboard** (`keyboard.ts`): WASD and arrows go up and down the menu and move the cursor on the map; Enter or Space confirms; Esc or Backspace goes back; P Wait; C Status; I the sheet of the ability in focus in the Abilities submenu; T turns the actor's facing; on the map Tab and Shift+Tab move the cursor between units, and with the menu, the abilities or "Execute?" open they walk its buttons, the 3D menu's included (a `menu` of `menuitem`s named by its head; the drawn focus follows, and the arrows move the DOM focus with the game's; Enter or Space press a button Tab reached: `pageKeeps`, Nov 18); in town Tab walks the menu button and the balloon; R and Shift+R rotate; Q and E zoom; F shows the whole map; H or ? Options and help; F3 counter; Z undo; PgUp and PgDn scroll the turn list.
- **Mouse:** hovering chooses, click confirms, right button goes back.
- **Gamepad** (Gamepad API, standard Xbox and PlayStation mapping): d-pad and analog stick move; A or × confirms; B or ○ goes back; X or □ Status; shoulders rotate the camera; triggers zoom; right stick up and down scrolls the turn list; Back undoes; Start opens the menu. With a dialog open, the gamepad moves between its buttons.
- **Key hints follow the input** (Nov 17, Fernando: gamepad icons in place of the keys): one table, `ACTIONS` in `glyphs.ts`, says for each action its keys and its pad buttons, and every hint, key cap and help line reads it (the battle's hint row, the keys beside the commands, the corner menu, Status, the town's prompt and balloon, "Execute?", the end board's primary, the title's and Options' lines, the town windows' foot, the help list). The pad's family comes from `gamepad.id` (`familyOf`: Xbox, PlayStation, Switch; unknown reads as Xbox) and each writes its own names by the button's place (a Switch pad's Confirm is its B). Options › "Key icons" (`dt:glyphs`): Automatic (a pad button brings the pad's icons, a key brings the keys back, the mouse changes nothing), Keyboard, or one family held. An action with no pad button (Wait, turn the unit, jump between units, the bar's numbers, whole map, fps) hides its hint while the pad's icons show. Drawn by code (`glyph-art.ts`, proportions in `glyph-art.json`): parchment key caps, pad buttons in gold and leather with a still glow. Lines of running text name the button in words.
- **Window lists** (`.camp-list`: title, Quick battle, town menu, Continue's save list, Options): up and down arrows move the focus (`dialog.ts`); in Options left and right change the focused row's value. Since Nov 16 `.arrow-walk` takes the arrows where the rows are not menu rows, and since Nov 17 (Fernando: down goes to what is below, sideways to what is beside) all four by where each button is on screen, staying at an edge: the HTML expedition map and the end of battle's commands (a place out of line counts while it is no farther to the side than ahead, since the map's floors do not line up). The 3D screens walk by what is seen too: the title and Options (`flow3d/`, the `walk` event), the Shop and the Tavern (`town3d/page.ts` `step`); in the 3D Options the commands (Restart, Town, Developer, Back) stand under the list, one column with it, so down from the last row reaches them and the column's two ends meet (one press up from the first row is Back; Fernando, Nov 17). Its categories open under the arrows, the pad and a tap; Tab only stops on one, and Enter (or Space, the pad's A) opens it and goes into its rows, so Tab reaches the rows of any category (Nov 18; opening on focus kept Tab from the rows of all but the last). In the 3D Party (Fernando, Nov 17) the arrows are on the roster or inside the member's page, never both: on the roster up and down choose the member and left and right turn the pages; Enter goes into the page, where the four walk its buttons by where they are seen; Esc (B) comes back to the roster, and from the roster leaves. The gamepad's d-pad is the arrow keys it stands for, never a walk through the buttons.
- **2nd tap** (`secondTap` in `dialog.ts`): Delete a saved game, Restart battle, Leave the room, Back to town from the map, a new expedition over a live one and Dismiss a recruit (Nov 16) say "Tap again: …" on the 1st tap; the label comes back after 5 s, when the focus leaves the button or on Esc (which then does not close the window).

The counter is drawn over its own node, which stays as its box and its on/off; the objective banner's node stays, unseen by opacity, as the `role="status"` the screen reader reads.
