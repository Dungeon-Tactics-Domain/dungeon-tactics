# Client: battle screen, windows and input

What the battle interface does and why, moved out of `src/client/CLAUDE.md` (the rules of code stay there). The [design system](design-system.md) is the source of truth for tokens and pieces; this file holds the behaviour of each screen and the request behind it. Decisions with their dates are also in [docs/decisions.md](../decisions.md).

## FFT battle-screen style

Fernando's decision, Nov 12: "discard the current interfaces and recreate the interface the same way as the original final fantasy tactics". The list below says what is implemented.

- **Turn screens** (`Screen` in `command.ts`): command menu → Move's map (choose the tile) or, on Abilities, the Agir primeiro screens (target → action → tile, below) → menu. Waiting leads to the direction choice on maps with direction.
  - Names, order and steps follow FFT: The Ivalice Chronicles (Fernando, Nov 16; captures in the labs' `refs/fft-ivalice`): Move · Abilities · Wait · Status (the row was "Act"; its id in code is still `act`); since Nov 19 (Fernando's grill of the new Agir) the order is Agir · Move · Wait · Status, the row named "Agir" because it opens the target, the abilities and the items; since Nov 19 ("Apagar tudo") the old abilities submenu, aiming on the map and the "Execute?" window are gone: Abilities always opens Agir primeiro, with "Undo move" under Move after a move (Nov 18; `menuRows`, so four rows at the turn's start and up to five). The reference's fifth row, Auto-battle, is left out: the game has no auto play for the player. Its Items and job-command lists inside Abilities are left out too: a unit's abilities are one flat list.
  - Move walks on confirming the tile (the reference's "Move Here"). The number 1 picks Move (the other numbers no longer pick abilities).
  - Cancel (Esc, Backspace, B, right button, the Back button) goes back one step, to the row it came from (`cancelStep` in `command.ts`, pure): lock step → tile choice (→ aim step) → action list → target list → Agir list → menu on Agir; from an ability or item picked on the Agir list: tile choice → its target list → the Agir list on that row; Move map → menu on Move; facing → menu on Wait; on the menu, it undoes a free move, else nothing.
- **Agir primeiro** (Fernando's grill, Nov 19; the only way to act since "Apagar tudo", Nov 19; the Options switch `dt:act-first` is gone): Agir opens its list first (Fernando's grill, Nov 19, variant A; `actList`): "Escolher alvo", the basic attack (the unit's first ability) straight to its targets, then "Técnicas", "Magias" (`magic` abilities) and "Itens", each left out when the unit has none (Fernando's grill, Nov 19, the third column). A group opens its actions in a third column: the Agir list slides left over the menu's place, its picked row lit, and the group's column opens where it stood; every deeper list does the same (the target list after Escolher alvo, the actions after a target), and Cancel runs it backwards (`menuView` `depth`, `parent`; `Column.shift`). A group row is greyed when none of its actions is on and counts the targets its actions hit on their own side. Each action row has its tag "principal" or "secundária" (the turn's two action slots) and, when it cannot be used this turn (no one reachable to hit, or its slot spent), greyed with the reason under its name; a row that is on also says, under its name, how many targets its target list will show (Fernando's grill, Nov 19). The list opens on the unit's last action while it is still on, else on "Escolher alvo" (`actStart`), and the Agir row always shows and opens, even with nothing left to do, to look at every action and its reach (Fernando, Nov 19): its note says why nothing can be done ("sem ações", "sem alvos", "na água") before two dots, a big one for the main action and a small one for the secondary, hollow once spent; the menu cursor starts on it only while an action is left (`canAct`). Every row of the list takes the cursor, an off one too: the focused ability shows its sheet beside the list (left and right or I turn its pages) and its reach around the unit on the map; Confirm or a click on an off row only plays the refusal sound. "Escolher alvo" is off with no one to reach. The menu and the list are each as tall as their own rows (`menuLayout`; the menu no longer grows when Agir opens). An ability or item picked there opens the target list of only who it can hit and then goes straight to its aim or tile step (`only` on the screens). That list keeps the action's side (`sideOf`; Fernando's grill, Nov 19): an ability with damage lists and counts only foes, a heal, an ally ability or an item only allies (the user too), with a last row "Escolher aliados" or "Escolher inimigos" that swaps to the other side when the action hits anyone there (`other` on the screen; Cancel swaps back); an ability with neither lists everyone. "Escolher alvo" opens the list of targets reachable by walking then acting this turn (`act-flow.ts`: enemies near to far, then allies, the user, then the area and self actions), drawn beside the menu with the head "Alvo"; each unit row says enemy, ally or you, its life and how far it is from the acting unit where it stands now, in the unit Move is shown in (half tiles on the halved map; Fernando, Nov 19). The list and the map are one choice: up and down walk the list, left and right walk the listed units on the map as they are seen (`neighbour`), and the mouse or a tap on a marked unit picks its row. Confirm opens "Ação": every ability in order and then the bag's items, an off one greyed with its reason on a line under its name (`MenuRow.below`). Then the tile choice: the tiles the action works from are marked, the best one first (staying put when it works), arrows walk them, the picked one shows its walk and the forecast strips, and the shot line and the counters the strike would leave owed, both drawn from the picked tile (`turnMarks`: `trajectory`, `countersOwed`; Fernando, Nov 19, "Trajetória e contra-ataques"); Confirm walks and acts in one go (`Game.runAct`). Tapping a listed unit or a marked tile picks it, twice goes on; Cancel goes back one screen. Keys and pad use the same screens (`act-turn.ts`). An ability that can land in more than one place (an area: on the target, or on the ground or a unit next to it whose area still hits it; `actAims`) first asks where it lands, the "Mira" step on the map (screen `aim`: the other aims marked, the focused one with the area it covers), and then lists only the tiles that aim there (Fernando, Nov 19). On the aim and tile steps an arrow goes to the nearest aim or tile that way on screen from where each one is seen (`neighbour` over the tiles' screen points, `ActHost.at`; a grid step was a slant on the diamond view), never by list order; the mouse over the map focuses the aim or tile under it, and a click or tap focuses without sliding the camera, so a 2nd click on the same spot goes on. A charge aimed at a unit asks first, in the list's place, where to lock it (on the character or on the tile, starting on the ability's usual lock; Cancel goes back to the tiles) and only then walks and declares it. A dual action (the Throwing knife, tagged "principal ou secundária") with both slots free asks last, the same way, "Ação principal · 100%" or "Secundária · 50%", starting on the main, the map and strips showing the half on the secondary (Fernando, Nov 20, "Escolha no fim"; `needsSlot`, `spotPreview`); with one slot free it takes that one without asking. The action list also shows the focused action's sheet beside it (Fernando, Nov 19, "Ficha da habilidade no Agir"; `menu3d/sheet.ts`): three pages, Description (reach, damage, hit), Effects and Cost, as the old abilities sheet had; left and right (or the sheet's two arrows, or a tap on them, 44 px on a finger) turn them around the ring, the I key and the pad's north button ("Details" at the foot) turn to the next, and another action opens on the first page. An item row has no ability facts: its sheet is one page, what it does (`itemDetail`: heal, mana, timed effect), so there is nothing to turn. The target and tile screens show no sheet. The help line says what to pick on each screen; on the action list it gives an item's detail (heal, mana and timed effect, `itemDetail`) and only the name of an ability, whose detail is on its sheet (Nov 19: the long line was tiny on a phone), and on an item's tile what it gives from there for these screens.
- **Target list by side** (Fernando, Nov 25: "no pick a target tbm deve ter a opcao de target allies ... em pick allies o fundo do icone deve ser azul ... qnd eh inimigos o fundo do icone deve ser vermelho"): Pick a target lists the enemies and the area aims, then a last row Pick allies; the allies' list (the user included) ends with Pick enemies, as the lists of one action already did (`actTargets`). A tap on a unit of the other side swaps the list to it, on its row. The icon of a target row stands on a blue plate for an ally and a red one for an enemy (`MenuRow.plate`, the `ally` and `foe` plates of `menu3d/models.ts`); the shield or sword icon keeps saying it without colour. A Pick allies row on the Agir list itself waits for the camera work in `act-turn.ts`.
- **What is not copied:** art, font, icons, portraits, frames, sounds and text of the original. Panels are drawn in 3D from our own tokens (the `--ui3d-*` tokens at the top of `style.css`, see the design system), portraits are our KayKit characters (`public/chars`, `scripts/portraits.mjs`), and our own ▶ arrow takes the place of the menu hand.

## BattleHud pieces

- **Top left corner:** the tile under the cursor (`tileInfo`) and the list of upcoming turns.
  - Since Nov 15 (Fernando's decision; the short list of 4 and its L key are gone) every unit of the battle in compact rows: 36 px face beside the number or NOW and the name with ● or ◆; a queued charge is its own row with the caster's face, the ability icon and "resolves in N" ticks.
  - Two rounds, each row with a small round mark 1 or 2. On the Agir tile screen it is a copy of the queue where the one acting pays the cost of acting (100 after moving, 80 without) and a charged ability's row sits where it fires. Pure parts in `turn-list.ts`; the projection is `previewOrder` with its `now` argument.
  - It scrolls within the column above the acting panel (wheel, PgUp/PgDn, right stick): a new turn scrolls back to NOW, a redraw keeps where the player scrolled, and the charge row of the aimed ability is kept in view.
- **Turn order on the map** (Nov 17, Fernando: "the turn order as a number near each character's health bar"): every
  unit carries the number of its next turn's row in the list (`turnOrder` in `turn-list.ts`, from the rows the list
  itself shows, so it follows the copy of the queue while aiming), glued to the left of its health bar (to its right while the
  turn list would cover it: `sideOrder`, every frame) and facing the
  camera with it (`setOrder` in `scene/unit-view.ts`). Big digits (`MARKER.orderH`, about 32 px at the whole-map zoom of
  a 1080p page) in the side's colour, blue for heroes and pink for enemies, gold for the one acting now; always on, for
  both sides. Beside the bar, so it clears the acting unit's arrow and stays above the head of the Brute and the
  Colossus (`tests/mark-style.test.ts`). A unit that never plays (the Beacon) has none; the town has none.
- **Painted faces** (Nov 15, Fernando's request): the unit panels and the turn list show the dialogue box's sheet (`faceSheet` in `talk.ts`) instead of the portrait, with an expression from the events `Game.animate` plays.
  - Pure rules in `battle-face.ts` (sheet by class, `faceCues`, `expressionAt`, tested); moods kept by `Game` on the battle clock (`BattleScene.now`); frames moved every frame by `BattleHud.showFaces` without a redraw.
  - The end-of-battle board shows a hero row per hero who fought, the fallen included (`resultBoard` in `game/end-screens.ts`, heroes from `endHeroes` in `tally.ts`: the roster, since the state drops the fallen), with a still face (`stillFace` in `hud-pieces.ts`) by `endExpression`.
  - Outside the battle the Party screen's list and Status head (`town-party.ts`) and the Tavern's recruit rows (`town-shop.ts`) show the class's neutral still face. Units without a sheet keep `portrait()`.
- **Mana and stamina** (Nov 16; the rules in `src/shared/CLAUDE.md`): the unit panels add an MP (mana, `--mp`) or ST (stamina, `--sp`) bar for the pool the sheet has (`poolGauges`), the Status window its left/max and regen (`poolRows`), the party sheet the full bars and the regen line (`regenLine`). Life regen from the pieces shows on Status beside the life ("210/210, +10 HP per turn") and in the same regen line; when it comes back at the start of a turn the unit floats a green number like a heal and the log says it as its own sentence ("Warrior +10 regen", `regen` event).
  - The Agir action list shows the cost and the charge ticks; a row it cannot pay is grayed out and says how much is missing (`shortOf`); the expedition map lists each hero's mana.
  - `newBattle` (`game/boot.ts`) sets the carried mana and gives the first actor the regen `Battle.start` spent on the full pool.
- **Top middle:** the help line and, below it, the ability name banner.
- **Beside the acting character** (Nov 13, Fernando's request: "beside the character like in FFT"): the Move · (Undo move) · Abilities · Wait · Status menu; the forecast (hit chance, effect, life before → after) is on the strike strips of the Agir tile screen.
  - Since Nov 15 the menu is at the lab kit's size: a head with the menu name, 60 px rows with the 32 px ability icon or a glass orb before the name.
  - `BattleScene.screenOf` gives the character on screen; `placeBeside` (`place-beside.ts`, pure and tested) picks the side (right; with no room, left) and slides in height so as not to cover the turn list (only the HTML menu, which the 3D menu stands for, uses it).
  - `Game.placeMenu` repositions every frame (only left/top, without redoing the DOM), because the camera slides and rotates with the menu open.
- **Undo the move** (Nov 13, Fernando's request, like Tactics Ogre; `canUndoMove` and `menuRows` in `command.ts`): after a free move an "Undo move" row shows right under Move and Esc (B, right button) on the menu undoes the move for free. Since Nov 18 (Fernando) it is a row of its own: Move stays while movement is left (it opens the range that remains) and leaves once none is; at the turn's start the menu has four rows, after a move with movement left five (Move, Undo move, Abilities, Wait, Status), and the menu grows upwards from its foot. After acting, Esc undoes a free move (one that drew no counter) and Z undoes the rest; Esc, P and Z do not repeat when held, and the menu cursor never starts on "Undo move" (on Move while movement is left, otherwise on Abilities or Wait).
- **Tap with slack** (Fernando, Nov 20, "Toque com folga"): a half-tile unit is about 20 px on a phone, so a tap or click that hits no drawn body picks the unit whose body centre on screen is nearest, within 22 px (half the 44 px target); a body hit still wins (`Units.pick`, `nearestTap` in `tap-slack.ts`, pure and tested). Every pick goes through `BattleScene.pick`, so the mouse, its hover and touch all get it.
- **Fixed corners on a phone** (Fernando, Nov 20, option A: "no mobile não deve ter os menus saindo 1 por cima do outro, todos devem ficar presos, os menus de ação devem ficar no canto inferior direito"): on a phone (`layer.phone`) the command menu stands in the combat bar's bottom right corner at the page's right edge, under the right thumb, its rows 44 px where the page has the height and never over the tile line above; the Agir list opens in the menu's own box instead of the cascade, and the action's sheet sits left of the columns (`cornerLayout` in `menu3d/menu-layout.ts`, pure and tested). A tablet and a PC keep the menu beside the acting sheet. The sandbox's `"touch": true` opens this layout at a phone-sized `viewport`.
- **Menu shut and another unit's mini menu** (Fernando, Nov 18 night, the phone grill): on the menu a tap or click on an empty tile shuts the menu and only moves the cursor and the tile line; the acting sheet (bottom left), the hero itself, Confirm or Esc bring it back on the same row, and with it shut the arrows walk the cursor on the map. On the open menu Esc shuts it only where it has nothing else to do (after a free move it still undoes it). A tap on another unit, ally or enemy, on the map or on its turn card, opens a look-only mini menu in the notice window (`unit-peek.ts`): Status (the unit's sheet), Abilities (each ability's range and cost) and Show range (where it walks in blue and could strike in red, `rangeMarks`, until the next tap or key). It never changes the hero of the turn or spends anything. Pure rules in `menu-state.ts`. The Agir screens keep their own taps: there a unit is a target.
- **Charges** (Nov 15, Fernando): confirming a charge aimed at a unit adds a lock step on the Agir tile screen (On the character / On the tile, starting on the ability's default lock; the two rows sit one above the other, arrows up and down go between them; Back goes back to the tiles; `needsLock` in `command.ts`).
  - Every charging unit, enemies included, keeps a line to what it is locked on. On the charging unit's Move screen the line is traced again from the tile under the cursor, with the help line naming who it would hit.
  - `charge-lines.ts` (pure and tested, from the rule that fires the charge) computes the lines; `scene/charge-lines.ts` draws them over everything, along the shot's own path (a cast spell's as a straight line). Past the point where the shot stops the line goes red with an X, orange on the caster's own side (friendly fire, half).
- **Shot flight** (the trajectory grill of Nov 15, from the Mechanics lab's prototype; the aiming preview of the path went with the old aiming screens on Nov 19, "Apagar tudo"):
  - The `attack` event carries the `Shot`, and `fx.ts` `fly` follows it up to the stop (`scene/unit-events.ts`), the tip on the path's tangent, a short trail and a spark where it lands, at the lab's speeds (the bow's curve slower than a straight throw or bolt); the struck unit's shake is the usual hit's. An attack without a path keeps the old lob.
- **Counters** (Nov 15, Fernando; redefined the same day: only in the turn of the strike, on walking out of the struck unit's reach or, for a shooter struck up close, out of its contact):
  - On the Move screen after a strike, the stops that draw a counter (out of a reach, or out of a shooter's contact and in its range, its line then ending at the stop) get a red frame (`counter` in `highlights`). On such a stop: the counter's line or arrow (`countersFrom`, from `pendingCounters`), the move help, and a note on the cursor with the Strike icon and the damage at half (`counterDamage`, `BattleScene.setCursorNote`).
- **Bottom:** panel of the acting unit (left) and of the unit under the cursor (right), with portrait and HP, MP and CT bars. Since Nov 15 at the lab kit's size (`--slot-w`); below 1600 px wide the portrait and the command menu shrink, because at the kit's size they covered the unit at 1280. The target panel is mirrored (portrait on the right).
- **Strike preview** (Nov 13, Fernando's request: "the attack preview interface should be like this one from Tactics Ogre (Reborn)"; our own design; since Nov 15 the lab kit's two-colour strip): on the Agir tile screen, beside the acting unit's panel, in the target panel's place, one strip per target (`strikes`, up to 3, the rest in "+N targets").
  - The action half on the panel blue: ability icon and name, the big damage −12–15 PTS (or +7–10 in green on a heal), ⊕ hit chance. The target half on its side's colour (wine for an enemy): name and side, the life bar with the part that goes away in stripes, and the `strikeInfo` line (↶ from behind / ⇥ from the side, push and spikes, "life 25 → 13–16", ALLY!).
  - One calculation (`forecast` in `forecast.ts`) for the strips and the turn list.
  - The strips go into `sizes.bottom` ; one or two stay within the acting panel's height, three rise about 100 px above it at 1920. Only the acting unit's panel counts in the height the scene discounts (`insets`): otherwise the map jumped when aiming.
- **Top right corner** (Fernando, Nov 18 night grill: no ☰ menu, buttons always in sight): the gear opens Options (H); left of it "↶ Desfazer", faded to 45% with no step to undo, and Back (↩), a finger's; the camera pair ↺ ↻ on the top left, right after the turn list. Undo and ↺ ↻ are on by default on touch and off on a PC (Options › Display › Screen buttons; `screen-buttons.ts`). Fast battle and Back to title are rows of Options (the fps counter moved to the debug panel on Nov 25); Show the whole map keeps only its key (F). The performance counter sits right below.
- **Marks** (Nov 17, Fernando): Options → Video and effects → "Marks": Simple, Light (default), Stone or Neon, saved in `dt:marks` and applied at once (`mark-style.ts`; `?marks=simples|luz|pedra|neon` in the address wins). What each one draws and what it costs: [client-scene.md](client-scene.md), "Mark styles".
- **Interface layout** (Nov 16, Fernando: the places of FFT: The Ivalice Chronicles): Options → Video and effects → "Interface layout", At the screen edges (default) or Centred 16:9 frame, saved in `dt:layout` and applied at once (`layout-mode.ts`; `?layout=edges|frame` in the address wins when the page opens). The places are the regions of `ui3d/layout.ts`. In the frame the HTML panels move in with it (same size); the 3D and CSS pieces below also take its scale. On a 16:9 screen of 1152x648 or more both look the same.
- **Only 3D** (Fernando, Nov 18): every piece is drawn on the shared 3D layer; there is no look flag and no HTML interface (`?ui=`, `?menu=`, `?turn=`, `?menu3d`, `?menucss` and the rest are gone). With no WebGL or a lost context the page shows one plain warning (`no3d.ts`). The client hands each window to its 3D screen through `looks3d.ts` (one slot per screen, filled when the screen is built).
  - Both 3D looks are screens of one WebGL layer over the HUD (`ui3d/layer.ts`), ticked by `BattleHud.showFaces` on the battle clock, drawn only while something moves. A lost context, the layer's or the map's (the scene's canvas, Nov 18), shows the warning of `no3d.ts` in place of the page.
  - The 3D menu takes each turn screen from the HUD (`menu3d/menu-view.ts`: the same rows, names and greyed states as the HTML menu, plus the Agir lists beside it; `changes()` names what to animate), sits where `menu3d/menu-layout.ts` says and brings its own transparent buttons (click a row, mouse over moves the focus). The HTML menu stays unseen for the HUD's own state.
  - The banner, the Height and the button hints (`hints3d/`, one screen of the same layer) draw the help line as the reference's pill (an ability's name takes it while it plays), the height of the tile under the cursor in the top right corner and, at the foot, what Confirm and Cancel do on this screen (`hintsFor`, from `Screen` and `cancelStep`) with the camera and menu buttons, for the device last touched. The help line, the tile line and the HUD's key hints stay in the HTML, unseen; the tile line itself is drawn small under the Height (Nov 16). Hints are one or two words, zoom and rotate as one "Camera" hint, and each help-line instruction fits one pill line (Fernando, Nov 16).
  - With the 3D bar on, the 3D turn list leaves out the acting card (the sheet's portrait is card 1, as on the reference), and the sheet's strip always leads with the class.
  - `window3d/tools3d.ts` draws the corner buttons (gear, Undo, Back, ↺ ↻) over the unseen HTML buttons, which keep their taps; `window3d/status3d.ts` draws the Status window (the C key, the Status command) as a parchment sheet in the middle of the darkened battle, over an unseen window of `dialog.ts` (`status-window.ts` hands it the sheet).
  - Both turn lists show each unit's life and CT beside its face (Nov 17, Fernando: "barra de vida com o número e o regen, barra de CT com o número e o ganho"): `unitStats` in `turn-list.ts` gives life, its max and `hpRegen` (life back at each of the unit's turns; only pieces give it), and CT (0 to 100) with the CT gained each tick, which is the sheet's `speed` with the pieces on (the game has no other "CT regen"). The values are the unit's now, repeated on each of its rows: the list projects the order, not the life. The 3D list has no keyboard or gamepad focus of its own, so the long wording (per turn, per tick) shows under the mouse and, for everyone, in the Controls window of the H menu (`game/options-help.ts`); the short lines are always on the card. The 3D card lost the thin life bar under its portrait (the one beside it carries the strike preview's blink), and the 3D bar's sheets show the life gain only (`bar-view.ts`; Nov 20, Fernando: no CT gain in the strike preview). Since Nov 20 (Fernando: "todos os stats ... apenas na primeira vez q eles aparecem no turn order"; grill: "Rosto menor e o nome") the 3D list's first card of a unit (the list's head counts, even on the bar's sheet) says its name, "HP 53/120 · MP 20/30" with the life bar under it, and "ST 30/30 · CT 40/100", with no gain per turn or tick (`cardLines`); each later card of the same unit is 60% of a first one and says its name only (`repeats`, `QueueCard.small`), never so small that it and its gap give a finger under 44 px (`TAP` in `queue-layout.ts`). The CT bar beside the card is gone; the HTML list and its tip keep the long wording. Since Nov 20 (Fernando: "deve exibir as 4 barras de status de recurso ... só tá exibindo a vermelha de hp") a first card draws a thin bar under each number, in the 3D sheet's colours (`bar3d/plates.ts`): life (green, with the strike preview's blink) and mana under the second line, stamina and CT (gold) under the third, two to a line sharing its width; a pool the unit lacks has neither number nor bar, and its partner takes the line's width (`cardBars` in `turn-list.ts`, `stBar` in `statsLayout`). The numbers stay, so colour is never the only signal. A repeat's card is unchanged.
  - **Folding** (Fernando, Nov 20: "turn order pode ser colapsável"): the list's title ("Turns ▾" open, "Turns ▸" folded) folds the list to its faces and order numbers, no lines or bars, the cards back to their own 52 design px, and opens it again; by tap or click on the title (its unseen box reaches up over the list's top padding and down over the gap so a finger gets 44 px), L on the keyboard (`fold` in `keyAction`, `ACTIONS`, `keyHelp`, its hint in the title). Remembered per browser in `dt:turnsFold` (`hud-queue.ts` `turnsFolded`, `foldTurns`); with no choice yet a phone opens it folded and a PC open (a tablet folded since Nov 22). On the gamepad it had the right stick's press (R3) until Nov 25, which went to the top view; the pad has no fold button now.
  - **Folded on touch** (Nov 22): with no choice yet, any touch device (`pointer: coarse`, a tablet too) opens the list folded; a PC opens it.
  - **See-through over a unit** (Nov 22, "Lista transparente por cima"): when a unit's middle on screen falls inside the open list's cards (`queue-layout.ts` `coversUnit`), the cards' ground eases to 45% so the unit shows; the lines' text stays opaque; Reduce transparency keeps them opaque.
  - **A charge's card** (Nov 22: "nessa turn order deve dizer q magia ela vai usar e em quais alvos"): line 1 "Explosão · 2 alvos" / "1 alvo" / "nenhum alvo", line 2 the targets ("Soldado, Arqueiro esqueleto", over two "Soldado +2", up to 3 small faces after them) or "na casa X 10 · Y 8" when nobody is in the area: whoever it would hit now (a unit lock follows the unit, a tile lock stays; `charge-lines.ts` `chargeCard`). Hovering the card, or tapping it on touch, lights its area and targets with the aim step's marks (`hud-queue.ts` `wireCards`, `HeroTurn.peekCharge`, `turn-marks.ts` `chargeMarks`). Folded, it shows face and ticks only.
  - The 3D turn list reads its cards from the same rows as the HTML list (`turn3d/queue-view.ts`) and takes their boxes from the HUD (`queueBoxes`), so the scroll, PgUp/PgDn and the strike preview's rows work as before; a tip beside the card under the mouse gives its name and life.
  - F3 adds a line with the layer's draw calls and triangles.

## Windows

- **Design system** (Nov 13, Fernando's request): tokens, pieces with their states, input, measured contrast and debts in [design-system.md](design-system.md). Change a token or a piece, change the document in the same commit.
- **One look for everything** (Nov 13, Fernando's choice; FFT Classic since Nov 15): `dialog()` windows on the same blue as the HUD panels: title, Options, Status, How to play, Credits, Party, battle end and Developer. ## Input

Keyboard, mouse and gamepad move the same cursor and go through the same `Game.runKey` (in the walkable town, `Town.run`).

- **Top view** (Fernando, Nov 25, as Tactics Ogre's tactical view; `scene/top-view.ts`, math in `top-view.ts`): T, the pad's R3 (glyph `r3`, named R3 on PlayStation and RS on Xbox and Switch) or the ⊞ button after ↺ ↻ (`hud-tools.ts`, drawn by `window3d/tools3d.ts`) ease the camera straight down at the nearest straight angle in 360 ms and frame the whole map (wheel and pinch still zoom; F frames it again); press again to go back to the tilt and zoom from before. From above R turns 90°, the arrows keep following the camera, the turn's hero does not pull the camera while the whole map shows, and an attack plays the action camera and comes back to the top. Each tile above height 0 shows its height (the Height corner's number) as a small number, one per whole tile when its half tiles agree, and the ground a light shade by height; both only from above. The scene's `top` (on, off, toggle, isTop) is what the map builder will reuse.
- **Keyboard** (`keyboard.ts`): WASD and arrows go up and down the menu and move the cursor on the map; Enter or Space confirms; Esc or Backspace goes back; P Wait; C Status; I (on the Agir action list) turns the action sheet's page, as left and right do; V turns the actor's facing (T until Nov 25); T the top view (below); on the map Tab and Shift+Tab move the cursor between units, and with the menu or an Agir list open they walk its buttons, the 3D menu's included (a `menu` of `menuitem`s named by its head; the drawn focus follows, and the arrows move the DOM focus with the game's; Enter or Space press a button Tab reached: `pageKeeps`, Nov 18); in town Tab walks the menu button and the balloon; R and Shift+R rotate; Q and E zoom; F shows the whole map; H or ? Options and help; F2 the debug panel (the pad: L3); F3 counter; Z undo; PgUp and PgDn scroll the turn list; L folds it to its faces and opens it.
- **Mouse:** hovering chooses, click confirms, right button goes back.
- **Gamepad** (Gamepad API, standard Xbox and PlayStation mapping): d-pad and analog stick move; A or × confirms; B or ○ goes back; X or □ Status; shoulders rotate the camera; triggers zoom; right stick up and down scrolls the turn list; Back undoes; Start opens the menu. With a dialog open, the gamepad moves between its buttons.
- **Key hints follow the input** (Nov 17, Fernando: gamepad icons in place of the keys): one table, `ACTIONS` in `glyphs.ts`, says for each action its keys and its pad buttons, and every hint, key cap and help line reads it (the battle's hint row, the keys beside the commands, the corner menu, Status, the town's prompt and balloon, the end board's primary, the title's and Options' lines, the town windows' foot, the help list). The pad's family comes from `gamepad.id` (`familyOf`: Xbox, PlayStation, Switch; unknown reads as Xbox) and each writes its own names by the button's place (a Switch pad's Confirm is its B). Options › "Key icons" (`dt:glyphs`): Automatic (a pad button brings the pad's icons, a key brings the keys back, the mouse changes nothing), Keyboard, or one family held. An action with no pad button (Wait, turn the unit, jump between units, the bar's numbers, whole map, fps) hides its hint while the pad's icons show. Drawn by code (`glyph-art.ts`, proportions in `glyph-art.json`): parchment key caps, pad buttons in gold and leather with a still glow. Lines of running text name the button in words.
- **Window lists** (`.camp-list`: title, Quick battle, town menu, Continue's save list, Options): up and down arrows move the focus (`dialog.ts`); in Options left and right change the focused row's value. Since Nov 16 `.arrow-walk` takes the arrows where the rows are not menu rows, and since Nov 17 (Fernando: down goes to what is below, sideways to what is beside) all four by where each button is on screen, staying at an edge: the HTML expedition map and the end of battle's commands (a place out of line counts while it is no farther to the side than ahead, since the map's floors do not line up). The 3D screens walk by what is seen too: the title and Options (`flow3d/`, the `walk` event), the Shop and the Tavern (`town3d/page.ts` `step`); in the 3D Options the commands (Restart, Town, Developer, Back) stand under the list, one column with it, so down from the last row reaches them and the column's two ends meet (one press up from the first row is Back; Fernando, Nov 17). Its categories open under the arrows, the pad and a tap; Tab only stops on one, and Enter (or Space, the pad's A) opens it and goes into its rows, so Tab reaches the rows of any category (Nov 18; opening on focus kept Tab from the rows of all but the last). In the 3D Party (Fernando, Nov 17) the arrows are on the roster or inside the member's page, never both: on the roster up and down choose the member and left and right turn the pages; Enter goes into the page, where the four walk its buttons by where they are seen; Esc (B) comes back to the roster, and from the roster leaves. The gamepad's d-pad is the arrow keys it stands for, never a walk through the buttons.
- **2nd tap** (`secondTap` in `dialog.ts`): Delete a saved game, Restart battle, Leave the room, Back to town from the map, a new expedition over a live one and Dismiss a recruit (Nov 16) say "Tap again: …" on the 1st tap; the label comes back after 5 s, when the focus leaves the button or on Esc (which then does not close the window).

- **Effects over the battle in 3D** (Nov 18, Fernando: the whole interface in 3D): the floating numbers, the cursor note, the objective banner, the victory confetti and the performance counter are drawn on the shared 3D layer by `hints3d/effects3d.ts` (piece `effects`; on the town's layer too). Each is handed over through `looks3d.ts` (`effects`).
