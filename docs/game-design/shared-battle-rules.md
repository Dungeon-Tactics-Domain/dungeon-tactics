# Shared: battle rules

The rules of a battle in `src/shared/battle`, moved out of `src/shared/CLAUDE.md` (the rules of code stay there): what each rule does, the request behind it and its date. Decisions with their dates are also in [docs/decisions.md](../decisions.md). Maps, units and half tiles: [shared-maps-units.md](shared-maps-units.md); the dungeon crawl: [shared-crawl.md](shared-crawl.md).

## Turns

- **FFT turn rules on every map** (Nov 12, Fernando's request): turn cost 100/80/60, tie-break by the list with enemies first, and actions with charge (`AbilityDef.charge`, `BattleState.charges`), sourced in `turn-queue.ts` (BMG 1.1 and A.2). A strong ability is slow because of its charge, not because of a delay.

- **Main and bonus action** (Nov 19, Fernando; action points come later, balance by playing): a turn has two slots, `turn.main` and `turn.bonus`, each holding the id used (`action-slots.ts`). An ability is `main` or `bonus` by its optional `action` field; without it, no damage (max 0) means bonus, else main. Every item is bonus. A main action needs the main slot free; a bonus action takes the bonus slot, else may spend the main one (two items, or a heal and a potion, in one turn). The AI follows the same rule: after its plan it takes a worthwhile main action if a bonus came first, then a worthwhile bonus (`extraAction` in `ai.ts`; deterministic, positive balance only). An old save's `usedAbility` loads as `main`. The Agir list tags each row "principal" or "secundária". A **dual action** (Nov 20, Fernando: the Throwing knife) has `bonusShare`: it is main at full damage or secondary at that % of it (50 for the knife; `slotFor`, `slotScale`; `Battle.act`'s `as` picks the slot). Without a pick it takes the main while free, else the secondary; asked for the secondary it never spills into the main. A save keeps it in the bonus slot; a dual action is never a charge.

## Act first

- **What a unit can do is worked out at the start of the turn** (Nov 19, Fernando's grill "Agir primeiro"; engine only, the interface comes later): `actionOptions(grid, state, id)` (`options.ts`) gives, for each of the unit's abilities (the basic attack first), the targets it can reach by walking and then acting inside the turn's movement budget (`turn.spent`, the same `reachable` as `Battle.reach`, step cost 1), and for each target the tiles it may act from (`Spot`: tile, walking cost, and the `Preview` from there). Targets: units, enemies before allies and near to far; then the user (`self`: centered or support abilities) and ground tiles around units for an area (`tile`).
- `previewAbility` takes an optional `origin`, the tile the actor would act from (a copy of the state with the actor moved), so range, sight, area, push and the hit chance read that tile; without it, where the actor stands.
- An ability that works nowhere is off with a reason: `spent` (with the pool and how much is missing), `range` (nothing reachable even walking), `sight` (reachable but the shot is stopped on the way), `no-target` (nothing to hit: a centered area with nobody near), `water` (deep water on every tile it could act from; with a shore within the walk, the action is on from there), `used` (no slot is left for its kind). Items come from `itemOptions` (below). The AI does not read it.
- Not simulated: the counters a walk would draw, and ground targets are only the tiles around each unit (one per whole tile on the split map), not every tile in range. No preview is asked for a pair of tiles too far apart to be in range. Measured on Nov 19, the worst hero of each of the six maps: 8 to 14 ms at the spawns and 14 to 20 ms with the teams close, on the half-tile maps the game plays; 2 to 5 ms on the whole-tile ones. `closest`, `gap` and `unitAt` (`pathing.ts`) became plain loops for it.

## Items

- **The heroes share a bag** (Nov 19, "Agir primeiro" grill, step 2): `BattleState.bag`, item id to count, absent = empty. A descent starts with 2 Life potions and 1 Ether (`START_BAG`), each chest adds 1 item rolled on its own stream from the node's seed (`chestItem`), and the bag goes into each room and comes back with what is left (`winRoom`).
- **The Item command** (`Battle.useItem`, `bag.ts`) is a bonus action (it fills `turn.bonus`, or `turn.main` when the bonus is taken, so two items fit in one turn) and reaches the user or an ally up to `ITEM_RANGE` = 3 whole tiles, measured between the two squares. No die: undo is free and gives the item back. Life potion heals 100 and Ether gives 30 mana, both capped at the sheet's max. `itemOptions` lists every item in the bag with whom it can reach after walking, or why it is off (`used`, `water`, `no-target`).
- **Timed effects** (`effects.ts`, `BattleState.effects`): Fire oil gives +10 physical power and makes martial hits fire; Stone potion gives +30% physical resistance and −1 whole tile of move. Each lasts 3 of the target's own turns, ticked at the end of its turn (on the user, 3 more after the turn it is used in); the same item again starts over. Combat (`withPower`, `defence`) and movement (`moveOf`: `Battle.reach`, `reachable`, `originsOf`) read them.
- The AI does not use items.

## Water

- **Water on every map** (Nov 12, Fernando's decision "like FFT", BMG 6.5): `Tile.depth` is optional; 1 = shallow, 2 or more = deep (`isDeep`).
- Entering water costs 1 point, like ground (Nov 13, Fernando's choice: fewer movement shackles; it used to cost 2). Allies pass through each other (you just can't stop on their tile); an enemy blocks.
- Since Nov 13 (Fernando's choice: "enters, but does not act inside"; in FFT only swimmers enter) deep water lets you enter and stop, and a unit in it can only walk or wait (`previewAbility` returns `water`).
- Shallow water does not lower anyone; in deep water the height of whoever is in it = `standH` (height minus depth), used for hit chance, line of sight, range and falls. The jump between tiles is measured by the surface (`t.h`), otherwise the bank would be 2 levels above the deep water.
- Movement, push, save and AI go through `canStand`/`stepCost`/`standH`/`isDeep` in `grid.ts`. A push stopped by deep water is our own assumption (kept on Nov 13).

## Spikes

- **Spikes** (night of Nov 12, our own rule): `Tile.spikes`; being pushed onto the tile (`simulatePush`) or ending your turn on it (`spikeAtTurnEnd`) hurts `COMBAT.SPIKE_DAMAGE` (50 since the x5 of Nov 15), `spike` event; walking over it does not hurt. The AI avoids stopping there and values pushing onto it for the damage.
- Where: the Flooded Crypt (letter `s`) and the crawl rooms (the theme's `hazard` in `src/content/crawl/rooms.ts`, 0 to 6 a room, see [shared-crawl.md](shared-crawl.md)).

## Corpses

- **A dead unit leaves a corpse that nobody may stop on** (Fernando, Nov 16; the Mechanics lab's `peso.json` reads "the corpse already exists in the rules"): `hurt` adds the dead unit's whole footprint to `BattleState.corpses` (the Colossus' 4 tiles, a token's 2x2 cells). Like a map body (`Tile.noStop`, Nov 13), a corpse cell is walked, jumped and pushed over but never ends a move or a push; `noStopAt` (`pathing.ts`) is the one reading, used by `reachable` and `simulatePush`, so the preview, the AI and the forecast agree. A save is refused when a corpse cell lies under a living unit (`parseBattleState`).
- Corpses stay to the end of the battle, are not units (no target, counter or turn) and roll no die. The field is optional: absent = none (a battle without deaths, a save from before); `parseBattleState` keeps cells inside the map, one per cell. Undo of the killing blow takes the corpse back.
- **A push passes over a body and only cannot end on it** (Fernando, Nov 16, the lab audit; before, a push stopped before the body with the bump while a walk crossed it): a corpse or a map body counts in the push's distance like any cell. A push that runs out with any cell of the square on a body slides back to the last cell where it could stop, before the whole chain of bodies, with no bump (nothing solid was hit); a wall, unit, edge, deep water or high step past the body still bumps, and the square ends on that same last cell before the bodies. The same holds for the half-tile extra cell that would settle a square on uneven ground: a body on it slides the square back to its last stop with no bump (before, it bumped for 25). Falls and spikes met on the way back out are dropped with the cells (`simulatePush` keeps the snapshot of the last stop, as for uneven ground), so the client never animates the unit over the body and back. `BattleState.corpseDefs` (Nov 19) keeps the unit that died on each corpse cell (by `posKey`) so a resumed battle draws that unit's own model; a save without it falls back to the stock knight and rogue. Corpses exist only in play: crawl rooms and their verifier do not change.
- Not done yet: a corpse does not block sight (a map body blocks 1 level, `LOW` in `grid.ts`), and after a reload no body is drawn on its cells (the state keeps only the cells; the scene draws the death as it happens).

## Shots

The trajectory grill of Nov 15, Fernando's realistic rule; the Mechanics lab's `trajetorias.json` and its prototype, whose path `traceShot` (`pathing.ts`) ports.

- A half tile is 1 m and a level 1 m (a whole map's tile is 2 m), so `traceShot` counts cells across and levels up. A flying ability (`needsSight`) leaves the middle of the shooter's body, half its height (`bodyMetres`: a person 1.7 m, so 0.85 m) above its floor (deep water: its surface), and aims at the middle of the target's body (a rat's 0.34 m: one on a ledge hides behind its edge); an empty aimed tile is aimed at a person's middle. Until Nov 25 every end stood 1.5 m up.
- **The shooter adapts and the shot hits any visible part** (Nov 25, Fernando: "um range que o personagem dependendo da sua forma como humanoid e quadrupede q se adaptem para realizar cada ataque"; picked "A primeira que passa", "Depende do ataque", "Acerta qualquer parte visível", "Menos visível, menos chance"). Each body has a form (`UnitDef.form`): a person launches from 50% to 95% of its height, a four-legged creature (rat, spider) from 70% to 100% (its head), a snake from 30% to 90%, a flyer (bat) from 10% to 100%. An ability may narrow that range (`AbilityDef.launch`, a share of it): the bow's shots its upper 60%, the thrown knife its upper half (an overhead throw). `traceShot` tries the middle, top and bottom of that range and, from each, points of the target's body at about 67%, 43%, 90% and 20% of its height; the first pair with a clear path is the shot drawn and used (`lift` is that point's height). With no clear pair the shot leaves the middle of the range for the target's middle and stops on the obstacle. `Shot.seen` is the share of the target's points that origin sees, and the aimed unit's hit chance becomes chance x (1 - weight x hidden share), rounded down (weight 1 by default, so a person whose head and chest clear a ledge is hit half as often). An empty aimed tile keeps the person's middle and no share. Each part is a switch with its numbers in `shot-rules.ts` (`SHOT_RULES`: launch, parts, cover, the form ranges, the cover weight), set from Options > Debug to compare balances; all switches off is the middle-to-middle rule above. The crawl room checks always use the defaults (`SHOT_DEFAULTS`), since a room is rebuilt from its seed. Cost measured on Ash Wood halved (`actionOptions` for one unit, average of 10, switches off then on): the Archer 14.5 to 17.2 ms, the Rogue 6.3 to 7.0 ms.
- The path is straight (the thrown knife, Firebolt, Gust) or a curve (`AbilityDef.arc`: the bow's three shots) whose top is 1 level per 3 whole tiles between the ends, at least 1 and at most 3, above the higher end, the lab's parabola. The curve passes over the bodies in its way on even ground; the straight shot stops at the first unit or obstacle (Fernando, Nov 16).
- It stops at the first thing the path crosses: a cell's terrain column (`blockTop`: ground plus wall 4, forest 2, a low object or a corpse 1) or another unit's body, as tall as it is (`bodyMetres`; 1.8 m for everyone until Nov 25) over its whole square (a token's 2x2, the Colossus' 4x4); only the shooter and the target are ignored, with no exception for a tile beside either end.
- On the two ends' own whole tiles only the ground counts (our own reading, where the lab never stands a unit on an obstacle): a unit in a forest still shoots and is shot at, the forest's cover counted in the hit chance as before, and a cliff face below the target still stops a shot from beneath.
- The crossing is exact (every grid line in order; along a seam both sides count, a corner touch neither), so A to B and B to A agree and the split map answers as the whole one. Terrain wins a tie, so a shot into a ledge hits the ledge, and a single-target shot stopped by terrain hits nobody.
- Blast, Heal and Shadow wave are cast on the target and need no path.
- The `Shot` rides on `Preview.shot` and the `attack` event (not in the state, so saves are unchanged): the same path gives the rule, the AI (`previewAbility`), charges and counters (traced from where the caster stands when they fire), the crawl room checks and the client's preview, charge lines and flight. The lab's guide for weapons (straight about +30% physical power, a curve +1 range) is content data, not a rule: in `src/content/abilities.ts` the bow's shots have +1 range and the thrown knife about +30% damage.

## Charge rules

Nov 15, Fernando.

- A unit may start a charge and still walk; starting it costs half the movement left, rounded up (`Battle.act` adds it to `turn.spent`: 5 cells left keeps 2), melee charges too, and after move and charge the free "Undo move" is gone (`lastFree` also compares the `main` and `bonus` slots).
- The lock is the player's: `act(ability, target, lock)` stores `Charge.follow` (null = the tile, else the unit it follows; `panel` is only the default lock, `defaultLock`), saved and checked by `parseBattleState`.
- The shot is traced when it fires, from where the caster stands then (`chargeAim` + `aimTile` + `resolveAbility` in `turn-queue.ts`), with the usual shot path: behind cover it stops on the obstacle, and the event shows that stop instead of a fizzle.

## Attributes in the hit and the damage

Nov 15, Fernando's grill; the Mechanics lab's `equipamento.json`, formulas section.

- **Hit chance** as before (the side and back bonus spells too), then x (1 - the target's dodge x `COMBAT.DODGE_BY_SIDE`: 100% from the front, 75% from the side, 50% from behind), rounded half up in whole numbers and never below 5% (`afterDodge`; Fernando's choice, where the lab clamps before the dodge), spells dodged too, heals never.
- **Flank damage by class** (Fernando, Nov 20): `UnitDef.flankDamage` gives a % more or less damage by where the hit catches the target (`facingSide`, the same side the hit chance reads), on every ability of the unit, its counter too (`landed`). Only the Rogue has it: −25% from the front, the same from the side, +50% from behind. The side and back hit bonus stays everyone's.
- **Damage** is the roll plus `physPower` on a martial ability or `magPower` on a spell, and a heal adds `magPower` too (Fernando, as the lab), then x (1 - physical or magic resistance) x (1 - the resistance to `AbilityDef.element`), the counter's half and the side share, rounded down, at least 1 (`landed` in `combat.ts`, whole numbers until the last division).
- A charging target loses `CHARGING_PHYS` 50 physical and `CHARGING_MAG` 25 magic resistance (FFT's x1.5 on the Charging status before).
- Resistances stay between -100 and 60 (`resistOf`), dodge at most 50 (`dodgeOf`). Heals and a heal on the undead ignore resistances.
- Push, fall and spike damage x5 with life (`BUMP_DAMAGE` 25, `FALL_DAMAGE` 45 a level, `SPIKE_DAMAGE` 50).
- The preview, the forecast, the counter note and the AI (`damageRange` for the threat of a nearby enemy) read the same calculation.

## Equipment in 7 slots

Nov 16, slice 3 of the Nov 15 grill; the Mechanics lab's `equipamento.json`. No XP, so every number past the class comes from pieces.

- A `Loadout` has main and off hand, head, body, legs and 2 accessories (`SLOTS`); an item has a `kind` (an accessory fits either accessory slot, `kindOf`), a rarity (common, rare, epic) and general stats (`STATS` plus `resist`), the same against everything.
- `bonusOf` sums the worn pieces (`worn` drops an unknown id or a piece in a slot of another kind) and `withGear` adds them to the class sheet with raw sums, so `resistOf` and `dodgeOf` still cap on read and the client's battle sheets (`dev-roster.ts`) count the equipment through the same rule.
- Move and jump in whole tiles; speed never below 1.
- Mana and stamina pieces raise the pool's max, which is also its start value (`vars`); a class without that pool gets one from the piece (Fernando, Nov 16: every class may have both resources, since later sub-classes will mix mana and stamina skills). Mana regen adds to the class's `regen.mana` when the sheet has a mana pool (with none it gives nothing) and life regen to `hpRegen` (10 a point); stamina regen stays the class's (the lab gives no piece one). `withGear` builds new `vars` and `regen` objects, so the class sheet never changes. The expedition carries mana against the geared max (`manaOf` in `game/crawl.ts` feeds `classMana`): a new max, from a class or a piece changed in town, enters full once.

## Friendly fire

- **No exceptions** (Nov 15, Fernando): every hit and heal lands on either side, aimed or as collateral; on the caster's own side damage is `COMBAT.FRIENDLY_FIRE` (half), and a heal on the other side heals half (`sideShare` in `combat.ts`), the same for enemies.
- `UnitDef.undead` (skeleton archer, minion, colossus): a heal hurts it by `COMBAT.UNDEAD_HEAL` (half) of the rolled amount instead, a `hit` event.
- Push, wall and fall damage are not halved. The preview, the forecast, the AI and the roll all read the same numbers.

## Counters

Redefined on Nov 15 after Fernando was struck by a unit he had not attacked that turn.

- A direct strike (not a charge, not on its own side) on a unit with a single-target basic attack (`canCounter`; an area one, radius above 0 like the Colossus' Shadow Wave, never counters for now, a save's owed one included), whatever the strike's kind, puts it in `turn.engaged`, so it lasts only the attacker's turn.
- Each move order of the attacker that starts in the reach of that unit's basic attack and ends out of it (`previewAbility` ok, so range with height; for a ranged one also closer than its minimum) draws its counter, once per foe per turn (Nov 16, Fernando: a foe that already answered leaves `engaged`, so out, back in and out again draws no second one): the basic attack at `COMBAT.COUNTER` (half), hit before it leaves, friendly fire and shot path included, with the attacker turned to face it so a counter never gets the side or back bonus.
- A shooter the attacker stands too close to (closer than its basic attack's minimum range, `inContact`, measured like the aim: the Archer, Mage, Skeleton Archer, Dark Mage and Acolyte against an adjacent attacker) cannot strike it there, so it fires once a move ends out of that contact and in its range, at the stop, after the walk (Fernando's choice, Nov 15: an archer answers the melee unit that hit it and walked away).
- `PendingCounter.at` says where each counter finds the attacker and `Battle.move` plays the start-tile ones before the walk event and these after it, clearing the free undo either way. Walking back into a shooter's contact leaves its reach, so out, in and out again draws 3 shots.
- `pendingCounters` is the one reading for the rule, the AI's retreat (`counterCost` in `chooseRetreat`, a death weighed like a kill) and the client.
- Saves: `parseBattleState` reads the Nov 13 objects in the turn and the short-lived Nov 15 `BattleState.engaged` (only the actor's strike of this turn, `turns` 2, still counts), dropping repeats, the actor's own side and ids no longer in the battle; a unit that dies leaves `turn.engaged` at once (`hurt`), since a friendly counter can kill another owed one mid-move and the save then named a missing unit.
- Known gaps (review of Nov 15, not fixed): a unit in deep water still counters, since the counter's preview passes `charged` and so skips the water check; the cursor damage reads every counter on the state before the move, so it can differ when an earlier counter of the same move kills a unit in a later shot's path.
- Facing (`UnitState.facing`) is on every map since Nov 13 (units spawn facing the nearest enemy; an older save is filled in), and counter-attacks (`counters` in `counter.ts`) work everywhere; only the counters of `UnitState.vars` (from `UnitDef.vars`) exist for scenarios that ask for them.

## Jump by class

- **Per-class jump** (Nov 13, Fernando's request: "jump mechanics like FFT"): `UnitDef.jump` (without it, `MAX_JUMP` = 1) says how many levels the unit climbs or descends in one step; every jump question goes through `jumpOf` (`pathing.ts`), which a scenario may swap for `rules.jump` (the crawl verifier does).
- Values: Lancer 3, Rogue and Minion 2, the rest 1 (`src/content/units.ts`; in FFT proportion, where the Thief jumps 1 more than the other classes; with Rogue 3 and archers 2, stage 5 ranged from 35% to 77%).
- **FFT gap** (`leaps`, same day, Fernando's request): flies up to `[Jump/2]` tiles in a straight line over deep water or tiles lower than both ends (a wall blocks), 1 movement point per tile flown; the AI sees it in `walkMap`. With jump 2 the Rogue crosses the moat of the Crypt Heart; in the FFT study (jump 3 for every class; removed Nov 16) the gap also counts, and the battle ended at 73% (300 seeds).
- A push still hits a step of 2 (`MAX_JUMP`, it is physics, not the jump of whoever is pushed).
- **On half tiles** (Nov 15, Fernando: "the move and jump values double; 1 per half tile") the jump counts in cells like the move, so a token's gap is up to J cells for jump J (`squareMoves`), 1 movement point per cell. That it lands level, wholly past the gap, is our own reading (Nov 15, kept by Fernando the same day): a 1-cell hop landed half inside a 1-tile trench, so a jump-1 archer crossed the Flooded Crypt trench it cannot cross on whole tiles. Climbing and dropping stay in height levels: a level is a physical step and has no half. With the level landing the AI's walking map on half tiles is exactly twice the whole one for every unit on every map.

## AI

- **AI and charge** (Nov 12; Nov 15): the Aimed Shot (default lock on the tile) is worth 40% against whoever plays before it fires (`W.ESCAPE`); a charge locked on the unit gets no discount. The AI scores hits on its own side at half like the rule, and moves out of queued charges' line of fire (`inLine`, `W.LINE`: chance times average damage of every charge that would hit it there); after acting, a charging unit's retreat also scores its own charge from the new spot (`chooseRetreat`).
- **The AI measures distance on foot** (`walkMap` in `ai.ts`, Nov 12): the unit's water cost and jump count; the straight line left units standing on neighboring rooftops. A half-tile token walks it as its square, with the same moves as `reachable` (`squareMoves`, Nov 14 review: a 1-cell walker took gaps the token cannot).
- **The AI attacks whenever it can** (Nov 25, Fernando: "msm no modo easy devem atacar sempre q disponivel"; there is no difficulty setting): in `chooseTurn` a stop that attacks always beats one that does not, and an attack counts as available when it does more good than harm before its charge's wait (`W.CHARGE` is left out of that check, still in the ranking; same in `extraAction`). Measured on six depth-1 crawl rooms before the fix: in 56 of 75 enemy turns a hero was in reach at 72 to 95% and the enemy only walked, since the heroes' threat is only weighed on attacking stops. Counting that threat on every stop too was tried and dropped: a unit with no hit hid out of reach and a 20x20 AI-against-AI battle hung at 300 turns (`tests/ai-attacks.test.ts`, `tests/battle-sim.test.ts`). A unit a charge is locked on still hides when it has no hit.
- **A swarm unit stays beside its kin** (`UnitDef.swarm`, Nov 20, Fernando's pick "Enxame chega junto": rats, spiders and bats, so an area catches several): `kinNear` in `ai.ts` counts the living swarm allies touching the stop (8 neighbours, by every cell of both bodies) and each adds `W.KIN` (half a life point times `LIFE`, about one and a half tiles of approach) to the stop in `chooseTurn` and `chooseRetreat`. In `chooseTurn` the bonus only picks among stops of the same kind as the best one without it (attacking or not), so it never turns a hit in reach into a wait; a unit that is not swarm plays exactly as before (`tests/swarm-ai.test.ts`). Initial weight, unmeasured.

- **Mana and stamina** (Nov 15 grill, built Nov 16; numbers from the Mechanics lab's `equipamento.json`, a starting point Fernando tunes by playing): a spell spends mana and a martial ability stamina, by `AbilityDef.cost` (`['mana' | 'stamina', amount]`, the study's `[counter, amount]` field); a unit's basic attack has no cost and a counter is free (it resolves `charged`). The pools are `UnitDef.vars` (`RESOURCES`; the start value is the max) and live in `UnitState.vars` like the study's counters; `UnitDef.regen` adds back at the start of each of the unit's turns (`regen` in `startNextTurn`), never above the max. Every unit has both pools since Nov 20, and the second one comes back 3 a turn (Fernando's pick "Regenerar as barras novas"; `tests/content.test.ts` checks every pool regens at least 3). **Life regen** (`UnitDef.hpRegen`, Nov 15 grill, 10 a point since Fernando's Nov 16 answer) comes back in the same `regen` call, never above the max life and never on the fallen (they left the list); it is not a heal (no magic power, no friendly-fire share; the undead gain it too, confirmed by Fernando on Nov 16), costs no roll and is announced by a `regen` event after the charges that fired before the turn, only when something came back. No class has it; pieces give it (below). `poolOf` (a key absent reads full), `canPay` and `pay` in `combat.ts` are the one reading: `previewAbility` returns `spent`, the AI (its pick through the preview, the threat through `canPay`) and the menu follow it. A charge pays when it starts (as the study's MP did) and fires free; a charge replaced by a new one is not refunded. A new battle starts every pool full, so stamina refills in every room; mana is carried by the expedition (below). `parseBattleState` fills a missing pool with its max and refuses one below 0 or above it.
