# Interface design system (Nov 13, 2026)

How the Dungeon Tactics interface is built: tokens, pieces, states, input and contrast. Read it together with `src/client/CLAUDE.md` (code rules) and [client-battle-screen.md](client-battle-screen.md) (what each screen does and why). The layout follows FFT and Tactics Ogre; art, fonts, icons and text are ours.

**Status:** this document describes the FFT Classic theme (Nov 15), ported from the design lab kit (`dungeon-tactics-lab/design/src/fft-kit.css`): blue gradient panels, light text with a hard dark shadow, yellow focus. It is the only theme since Nov 15 (decision log in `docs/decisions.md`).

**Reading copy:** Design System artifact at https://claude.ai/artifact/RR7xTnyBPxE7GyUd1XysSK (Fernando's choice, Nov 13). Change a token or this document: republish `project/tokens.json` and `project/README.md` there.

**Source of truth:** the theme blocks at the top of `src/client/style.css` (tokens, see Themes) and the functions in `icons.ts`, `dialog.ts`, `hud-pieces.ts` and the other files each piece names in its "Lives in" column (`hud-menus.ts`, `hud-tools.ts`, `portrait.ts`, `talk.ts`, `town-ui.ts`, `game/end-screens.ts`, `game/crawl.ts`…). Every element is built with `el(tag, props, ...children)` in `icons.ts` (`on*` props become listeners; no `innerHTML` in the client). Every text button goes through `button(cls, label, onclick, props)` in `icons.ts`, except the two pieces that own their button: `choice()` (`aria-pressed`) and the dialogue box (`talk()`, the whole box is the button); the stylesheet is grouped by piece, one `/* ---- … ---- */` section each. This document describes; if it disagrees with the code, the code wins and the document is fixed in the same commit.

**Rules of use**

- Colour and size only through `:root` tokens. Exceptions are listed under "Debts".
- A new screen is built from the pieces below. Something missing: first one more parameter on the piece; a new piece only if that does not fit, recorded here.
- Colour is never the only cue: always text, a drawing or a mark with it (✓, ▶, stripes).
- All text through `t('pt', 'en')`.
- Web on PC first, and phone and tablet are also a priority (Fernando, Nov 16, after the tablet could not be played). Screens: 1280×720 and 1920×1080 first, wider screens in section 10. Section 8 holds the touch rules and marks what holds today and what is still a target; new and changed interface follows them, and no change may break what section 8 marks as holding today. Decided Nov 16: the Back button in battle beside the menu button, a "Rotate your phone" notice on portrait phones under 600 px, the fps counter off by default, a Skip button on the dialogue box.
- Accessibility (section 9) and wide screens (section 10) come after the dated sections 5 to 7.

## 1. Tokens

### Themes (Nov 15)

The interface swaps its look through themes (Fernando's request: FFT Classic as the first theme, more later). A theme is only a block of tokens; layout and pieces stay the same.

- **Where:** `:root, :root[data-theme="fft"]` at the top of `style.css` is FFT Classic, the default and the base every other theme inherits from. Another theme is a `:root[data-theme="<id>"]` block right below it that redefines only the tokens it changes. Outside these blocks, CSS and code use tokens only and never name a theme.
- **Choosing:** `src/client/theme.ts` holds `THEMES` (id, Portuguese and English name; the first is the default), `getTheme()` and `setTheme(id)`. The choice is saved in `dt:theme` and set as `data-theme` on `<html>`: an inline script in `index.html` copies the save before the first paint, and `theme.ts` replaces an unknown value with the default on load. Options → Video and effects → Theme lists `THEMES` with `choice()` (one entry today); switching applies at once, no reload.
- **Adding a theme:** one entry in `THEMES`, one `:root[data-theme="<id>"]` block in `style.css`, the contrast table below measured for its pairs. A value that is a literal inside a piece becomes a token in the base block first (with its current value), so the new theme only overrides tokens.
- **Kit names:** the kit's `--font-px` is `--font-title` here (both point to `--font`).
- **Font:** Alegreya Sans hosted in `public/fonts` (`@font-face` under the token block; SIL OFL 1.1, licence beside the files). No Google Fonts.
- **Aliases on purpose:** some tokens share a value today (`--fg` = `--ink` = `--on-accent`; `--muted` = `--ink-muted`; `--trim-hi` = `--accent` = `--focus`; `--stone-lo` = `--paper-lo`; `--paper-white` = `--stone`). Each keeps its own name so a theme can split them; use the one that names the role.
- **Literals inside composite tokens:** `--glow` repeats `--focus` as `rgba(255,215,94,.55)`, `--focus-halo` repeats `--bg` (`#0a1028`), `--ink-shadow` and `--paper-frame` repeat `--shade` (`#050a18`). A theme that changes `--focus` or `--shade` must redefine these too (listed under Debts).

### Panel glass (Nov 16)

Fernando's request: an opacity slider for the blue interface, with a blur of what shows through it (glassmorphism). Default = the opaque look, with no extra layer: the town menu, the battle HUD and Options at 100% gave 0 different pixels against the stylesheet from before the change, at device pixel ratio 1 and 1.25 (Nov 16; the 3D canvas and the fps counter hidden, animations off).

- **Tokens:** `--glass-hi` `--glass` `--glass-lo` (the gradient stops), `--panel-veil` (layer under them), `--panel-rim` (a line in `--paper-frame`) and `--panel-filter` (the backdrop filter). Their `:root` defaults are the opaque look: the stops are plain `var(--paper*)`, veil and rim `transparent`, filter `none`. Below 100%, `src/client/glass.ts` sets them on `<html>`: each stop `color-mix(in srgb, var(--paper*) N%, transparent)`, the veil `--shade` at 75%, the rim `--paper-edge` at half the transparency, the blur. The `--paper*` tokens stay opaque: borders, portraits, the queue mark and the dev footer still use them.
- **Where:** every blue surface paints `linear-gradient(var(--glass-hi), …), var(--panel-veil)` and takes `backdrop-filter: var(--panel-filter)`: windows (`.dialog`, so Options, Shop, Tavern, Party, the town menu, saves, end windows), the result board's cards, the title's menu, the expedition legend, every HUD `.panel` (turn list, unit panels, command menu, help line, the dialogue box, the town's place tag, prompt and action balloon), the menu button and the strike preview's blue halves. Opaque on purpose: the focus band, buttons, fields, the wine enemy half of the strike preview and the defeat band (which also resets the filter). Windows with no background of their own (title, result board, the dark narration) reset the filter to `none`.
- **Why the defaults are plain:** a `color-mix()` stop would make the gradient interpolate in oklab (pixels moved at 100%), and `linear-gradient(in srgb, …)` drops the whole background in browsers without gradient colour spaces; a veil painted at 100% showed at the frame's edge at 125% scaling. So at 100% nothing new reaches the page; with glass on, the translucent gradient interpolates in oklab (only the middle of the gradient, a hue shift too small to see).
- **Options → Video and effects:** "Panel opacity" (`slider()` from 40% to 100% in steps of 5) and "Blur behind the panels" (`choice()`: Off, Light 6 px, Strong 12 px, both with `saturate(1.2)`), with a line holding every notice that applies: that the browser cannot blur, that the system asked for less transparency, or else when the blur shows. Saved in `dt:glass` (`{alpha, blur}`), read back by `parseGlass`, which falls back field by field.
- **States:** the filter is `none` while the panel is opaque (an opaque panel hides its backdrop, so the blur would cost GPU for nothing, and a filter makes the window a containing block for its fixed children). Off as well when the browser has no `backdrop-filter` (`CSS.supports`, with the `-webkit-` name) and under `prefers-reduced-transparency: reduce`, which also forces 100%. Touch screens (no `(hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)`) start with the blur Off; a mouse starts on Light, invisible until the opacity drops. Nothing animates, so `prefers-reduced-motion` has nothing to turn off.
- **Look:** `--paper-frame` gained a glass rim, a 1 px light inner line of `--paper-edge` at half the transparency (0 at 100%, 30% at the 40% floor); the static inner top glow is the sheen and `saturate(1.2)` does not scale (our choice: a wash over the top would lighten `--paper-hi` and eat the contrast margin). Below about 70% the panel reads as dark tinted glass, not a lighter blue: the veil is what keeps the text legible over a bright sky.

### Focus

- 3 px ring in `--focus`, offset 3 px, on every button, form field (text, number, select, checkbox, `details` summary) and the volume bar (`:focus-visible`).
- On the blue panels the yellow ring alone exceeds 6:1. **Outside the panels** (menu button and Back button over the scene, title language button, the town's action balloon, dialogue box) `--focus-halo` is added: a dark band hugging the ring, so it shows on the bright sky (18.8:1) and on the grass.
- Menu rows do not use a ring: focus is the ▶ and the light background (`.focus`), moved by the same cursor from keyboard, mouse and gamepad.
- A dialog that rebuilds itself after an action (Sheet, Equipment) returns focus to the button with the same name (`aria-label`, else its text), else to its first control: by position it landed on another button once a row came or went (Nov 16).

### Disabled

- One look: buttons in dialogs faded to 50%; menu rows in `--ink-off` without the text shadow on a sunken `--row-off` band (6.8:1, still readable; the band also tells a focused disabled row from a focused active one); no dark grey.
- A disabled button says why in its own text, in the line above it or in small type under it ("20 short" under Buy, "Party is full: dismiss someone on their sheet", "Only this one goes to battle" under Leave at camp).

### 2nd tap

- An action that loses something (Delete a saved game, Restart battle, Leave the room, Back to town from the map, a new expedition over a live one, Dismiss) only arms on the 1st tap: its label becomes "Tap again: …" (`secondTap` in `dialog.ts`). It disarms back to its label after 5 s, when the focus leaves it, or on Esc, which then does not close the window (Nov 16).

## 8. Touch and mobile rules (Nov 16)

Written on Nov 16 from the touch audit (headless Chrome with touch emulated, `isMobile` and `hasTouch`, at 820×1180, 1180×820 and 390×844 on master `1c63faf`; every flow driven with taps only; details in `_notas/auditoria/D-toque-mobile.md`, outside git). Fernando reported that the tablet could not be played. Whether "the tablet (768 px and wider, both orientations) plays start to finish with the fingers; a phone at least opens and plays" becomes a gate for every change is his open decision. Each rule says **Holds today** or **Target**; a target's measured breaks are its debt until fixed.

1. **Every target is at least 44 × 44 px** (Target). Buttons, menu rows, list buttons, icon buttons, form fields (a checkbox sits inside a label of at least 44 px) and links inside text (padded to 44 px of height). The size is the box the finger can hit (`getBoundingClientRect`), not the drawn glyph; two targets closer than 8 px count as one. Use `--tap`, never a literal under it. *Breaks (measured):* language button 86×32, Buy and Recruit 112×36, corner menu rows 40 to 42 px (38 px `min-height` plus the border), the Credits link 20 px tall, Developer checkboxes 13×13 (232 of its 254 controls under 44 px); *read:* "Execute?" Yes/No rows at 40 px.
2. **The map is the one exception, covered by two taps** (Holds today). A half tile is smaller than a finger (about 32 × 22 px at the default zoom on 1180×820, measured), so a tap never acts at once on the map: the first selects the tile and shows what a mouse hover shows (path, strike preview, counter note, tile line), a second tap on the same tile confirms (`main.ts` tap handler, `interaction.ts`). Pinch zooms the camera.
3. **Every action the keyboard needs to finish a turn or leave a screen has a visible control** (Target). Esc, B and the right button are shortcuts; a long press or a two-finger gesture is never the only way. *Breaks (measured):* on the Move and Act maps the only control on screen is the menu button and a long press does not go back (read: `hud.ts` declares `cancel()` in `HudActions`, no control calls it; the right-button `contextmenu` back is never fired by a long press on iPad Safari, deduction); a conversation skips only with Esc; the fps counter toggles only with F3.
4. **Text is at least 14 px on a touch screen** (Target); body stays at `--fs` and notes at `--fs-sm`. `--fs-xs` (12 px) is for pieces that show only with a keyboard and mouse (`.kbd`, `(hover: hover)`). *Holds:* the key hint and the key chip hide on touch (measured). *Breaks (measured):* the turn list's round tag and the fps counter at 12 px.
5. **Contrast: 4.5:1 for text and 3:1 for marks** over the worst background the piece can sit on (section 9). A disabled control keeps its reason readable in a line outside the faded control ("20 short" pattern, holds in Shop and Tavern). *Breaks (computed from the tokens):* a closed expedition room's reason fades with its button (about 3.4 to 3.8:1); the fps counter over a white sky 4.1:1. (The open room's enemies line was fixed on Nov 16: `--on-accent`, 4.9.)
6. **Safe areas** (Target). `index.html` sets `viewport-fit=cover`, so the page runs under the notch, the rounded corners and the home bar; every piece anchored to an edge adds `env(safe-area-inset-*)` to `--inset` (one token per edge, for example `--inset-b: calc(var(--inset) + env(safe-area-inset-bottom))`) and `.overlay` pads by the same. *Read:* no `env(safe-area-inset-*)` anywhere in `style.css`.
7. **Both orientations work; nothing locks or asks to rotate on a tablet** (Holds today, with breaks). The layout follows width and height, never a device name; every window fits the screen or scrolls inside itself (`max-height` in `dvh`, `overflow: auto`); the page never scrolls sideways at 360 px; a long window opens at its top with the ✕ in view. *Holds (measured):* both orientations opened at all three sizes, `scrollWidth` equal to `innerWidth` everywhere. *Breaks (measured):* the expedition map keeps the title's `overflow: visible` and its Back to town falls off screen (y 1106 on an 844 px phone; 774 px of content in a 738 px window at 1180×820); How to play opens scrolled to its end with the ✕ off screen.
8. **Nothing lives only in hover** (Target). Every `:hover` style sits inside `@media (hover: hover)`; information in a `title` tooltip also shows on screen or is not needed; copy that names a key or the mouse has a touch wording picked by the same `KEYS` test as the hints. *Breaks (read):* `:hover` outside the media query on the window ✕, the title list rows, the Options sections, Continue's where-to line and the Party rows; `title`-only hints (hit chance ⊕, "Round N" in the turn list, icon buttons); key wording on touch in the title hint, the conversation ("Enter next · Esc skip", measured at 820), the town prompt, "Town menu (Esc)" and the facing help.
9. **Gestures** (Holds in part). One finger dragging on the map pans the camera past an 8 px slop (`TAP_SLOP`, `touch-input.ts`), two fingers zoom the camera, a tap selects; `body` has `touch-action: none` and `user-select: none`, buttons `touch-action: manipulation`. *Target:* `touch-action: pan-y` on every scrolling box (`.dialog`, `.queue`, `.menu-body`, `.save-list`), because a scrolling box starts a new `touch-action` chain, and `overscroll-behavior: none` on `html` and `body` (pull to refresh can reload the page on Android Chrome; deduction). *Breaks (measured):* a pinch over How to play zoomed the whole page from 1× to 5× at every size; the same pinch over the map and over Credits (a window that does not scroll) stayed at 1×. Finger scrolling works in the turn list, Options, How to play, Shop and Party.
10. **Screen bands.** A new rule picks one of the breakpoints the CSS already uses instead of a new number:

    | Band | Size | What changes today |
    |---|---|---|
    | Phone | up to 600 px wide | Options sections on top (`.menu-layout` in one column) |
    | Narrow | up to 760 px wide | the Result board's hero cards wrap; the Sheet's two columns become one |
    | Narrow tablet | up to 900 px wide | town windows open over the town menu with a veil (side by side needs 901 px); the title's rows may wrap |
    | Tablet landscape and small PC | 901 to 1599 px wide | town windows beside the menu; battle menus at the compact size (44 px rows, submenu 320 px) |
    | PC | 1600 px wide and up | the lab kit's full sizes (60 px menu rows, command menu 422 px, submenu 460 px) |
    | Short screen | up to 700 px tall | the title's foot follows the menu |

    *Target (not in the CSS):* a phone band for landscape under 500 px tall; every battle piece with a fixed width (`.confirm` 460 px, `.abilities` 320 px, the strike strips) takes `min(<width>, 100vw - 2 × var(--inset))`; a piece sized in `vw` has a floor that keeps its content readable. *Breaks (measured):* at 390 px the submenu reaches x 504 and "Execute?" x 472; at 820×1180 "Execute?" covers the turn list; the unit panel at 35vw leaves its HP and CT bars 131 px wide at 1180, 5 px at 820 and 2 px at 390, and at 390 the two panels overlap.
11. **How to test a touch change.**
    - Emulate a touch device, not only a size: Playwright with `isMobile: true, hasTouch: true` (a resized desktop window keeps `(hover: hover)` and `(pointer: fine)`). Before measuring, assert that `matchMedia('(hover: none)')` and `matchMedia('(pointer: coarse)')` match and that `.hud` has no `kbd` class.
    - Sizes: 820×1180 and 1180×820 (tablet), 390×844 (phone), 360×640 for the smallest phone.
    - Drive the flow with `touchscreen.tap` only: title, New game, town, a talk, the town menu, the quest, a room, Move, Act, a target, "Execute?", Wait, the facing. A step that needs a key breaks rule 3.
    - Scroll with a real finger drag (`Input.dispatchTouchEvent` through CDP), not by setting `scrollTop`.
    - Measure every visible `button, a, input, select, summary, [role=button], [tabindex]` with `getBoundingClientRect` (rule 1), every visible text's computed `font-size` (rule 4) and `document.documentElement.scrollWidth` against `innerWidth` (rule 7).
    - Sound off: launch Chrome with `--mute-audio` and leave the music parameter out; any value of `?music` (or the old `?musica`, even `?musica=0`) turns off the automation mute (`AUTOMATED` in `sound.ts`).
    - Emulation does not show iPadOS Safari: no `contextmenu` on a long press, no focus on a tapped button, sticky hover, audio that unlocks only at the end of a tap. A touch change is done only after one pass on a real iPad (Safari) or Android tablet (Chrome).

## 9. Accessibility (Nov 16)

What the code does on master today, and the gaps the audits found (computed or read on Nov 16, not seen on screen unless said). A new piece keeps every "holds" below.

- **Focus is always visible** (holds). A 3 px `--focus` ring, offset 3 px, on `button`, `input`, `select` and `summary` (`:focus-visible`); outside the panels the dark `--focus-halo` hugs it (menu button, title language button, dialogue box); menu rows show focus as the band and the ▶, text and mark besides colour. A window opens with the focus inside it, `#app` behind is `inert`, and the Sheet returns the focus to the same button after an action. *Gaps:* the town's action balloon (`.town-act`, a button over the scene) has no halo, so its ring falls to 1.4:1 on a white sky; links (`.dialog a`, Credits) have no `:focus-visible` rule and keep the browser's default ring (not measured); on the title, closing a window does not return the focus to the row that opened it (Nov 16 windows review).
- **Reduced motion** (`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`; holds for the interface). Every CSS animation in Motion stops, the confetti does not fall, the battle behind the title stops turning and the painted mouth stays still. *Gaps:* the damage number still jumps in steps instead of standing still (`steps(1, end)` over 1.6 s); the camera shake on a heavy hit and the action camera's zoom and turn on every strike (`scene/units.ts`, `scene.ts`) ignore the preference, and Options has no switch for them. Following the system alone or adding an Options item is Fernando's open decision.
- **Reduced transparency** (`prefers-reduced-transparency: reduce`). Master reads it nowhere: panels and windows are opaque, and the translucent pieces are veils and bands whose text was measured over its worst background (`--strike` at 82%, the ability banner at 88%, the fps band at 55%, the window veil). Rule: a new translucent surface ships with an opaque fallback under this query and its contrast computed at its most transparent setting over a white sky (Panel glass, under Themes, does both in its branch).
- **Contrast minimum.** Text 4.5:1; large text (24 px, or 18.66 px bold) and non-text marks 3:1; over the worst background the piece can sit on (the white sky for anything over the scene); colour is never the only cue. Measured pairs are in section 1. *Gaps (computed from `:root`):* the corner menu's key hint on a hovered row, 4.3:1 (`--ink-muted` on `--row-hi`; the Party row and the window list already turn their muted text `--ink`); the fps counter over a white sky, 4.1:1; the title's foot (`--fg` at 0.85 opacity on the radial veil), about 4.2:1 (the veil's opacity there is a deduction from the gradient); the enemy ◆ in `--enemy` on the panel blue, 2.2:1, and the enemy's side stripe and face frame, 2.6:1 (non-text, and the word ENEMY carries the side; `--enemy-hi` would give 4.1 but changes the enemy's tone, Fernando's choice). A disabled control is exempt from the bar, so its reason goes in text outside it (Disabled).
- **Text size.** 16 px minimum on a PC and 14 px on a touch screen, with the exceptions listed under Typography; the interface has no text size setting.
- **Names and roles** (holds, read). Icon buttons are named by `title` and `aria-label`; decorative symbols and menu heads are `aria-hidden` (the menu's `aria-label` names it); the corner menu is a `menu` of `menuitem` rows with a `menuitemcheckbox`; `choice()` uses `aria-pressed`; the objective and ability banners are `role="status"`, the help line and the dialogue text `aria-live="polite"`. Never checked with a screen reader.
