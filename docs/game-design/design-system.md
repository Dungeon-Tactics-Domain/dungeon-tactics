# Interface design system (Nov 13, 2026)

How the Dungeon Tactics interface is built: tokens, pieces, states, input and contrast. Read it together with `src/client/CLAUDE.md` (code rules) and [client-battle-screen.md](client-battle-screen.md) (what each screen does and why). The layout follows FFT and Tactics Ogre; art, fonts, icons and text are ours.

**Status:** this document describes the FFT Classic theme (Nov 15), ported from the design lab kit (`dungeon-tactics-lab/design/src/fft-kit.css`): blue gradient panels, light text with a hard dark shadow, yellow focus. It is the only theme since Nov 15 (decision log in `docs/decisions.md`).

**Reading copy:** Design System artifact at https://claude.ai/artifact/RR7xTnyBPxE7GyUd1XysSK (Fernando's choice, Nov 13). Change a token or this document: republish `project/tokens.json` and `project/README.md` there.

**Source of truth:** the theme blocks at the top of `src/client/style.css` (tokens, see Themes) and the functions in `icons.ts`, `dialog.ts`, `hud-pieces.ts` and the other files each piece names in its "Lives in" column (`hud-menus.ts`, `hud-tools.ts`, `portrait.ts`, `talk.ts`, `town-ui.ts`, `game/end-screens.ts`, `game/crawl.ts`…). Every element is built with `el(tag, props, ...children)` in `icons.ts` (`on*` props become listeners; no `innerHTML` in the client). Every text button goes through `button(cls, label, onclick, props)` in `icons.ts`, except the two pieces that own their button: `choice()` (`aria-pressed`) and the dialogue box (`talk()`, the whole box is the button); the stylesheet is grouped by piece, one `/* ---- … ---- */` section each. This document describes; if it disagrees with the code, the code wins and the document is fixed in the same commit.

**Rules of use**

- Colour and size only through `:root` tokens. Exceptions are listed under "Debts".
- A new screen is built from the pieces below. Something missing: first one more parameter on the piece; a new piece only if that does not fit, recorded here.
- Colour is never the only cue: always text, a drawing or a mark with it (✓, ▶, stripes).
- All text through `t('pt', 'en')`.
- Web on PC first, and phone and tablet are also a priority (Fernando, Nov 16, after the tablet could not be played). Screens: 1280×720 and 1920×1080 first, wider screens in section 10. Section 8 holds the touch rules and marks what holds today and what is still a target; new and changed interface follows them, and no change may break what section 8 marks as holding today. Decided Nov 16: the Back button in battle beside the menu button, a "Rotate your phone" notice on portrait phones under 600 px, the fps counter off by default, a Skip button on the dialogue box.
- Accessibility (section 9) and wide screens (section 10) come after the dated sections 5 to 7.

## 1. Tokens

### Tokens (Nov 18: one set, no themes, no blue)

The `:root` block at the top of `style.css` holds every token. The swappable themes (`theme.ts`, `dt:theme`, Options → Theme, Nov 15) and the panel glass (`glass.ts`, `dt:glass`, Options → Panel opacity and Blur behind the panels, Nov 16) were removed on Nov 18 with the HTML look they dressed. With it went the blue panel tokens (`--paper*`, `--glass*`, `--stone*`, `--frame`, `--focus-band`, `--well`, `--shade`, `--overlay` and the rest no rule read any more): the interface is only 3D, drawn with the `--ui3d-*` tokens, and the HTML under it is an unseen skeleton whose borders keep their width with no colour.

- **Font:** Alegreya Sans hosted in `public/fonts` (`@font-face` under the token block; SIL OFL 1.1, licence beside the files). No Google Fonts. The 3D pieces use `--font-ui3d` (a system serif).
- **Read by name from TS:** the 3D code reads some tokens with `getComputedStyle`: `--ui3d-*` (`ui3d/layer.ts`, `town3d/`, `result3d/`, `flow3d/`), `--mark-*` (`flow3d/sheet-draw.ts`), and `--fg`, `--heal`, `--float-*`, `--strike`, `--strike-dmg`, `--mark-counter`, `--font-title` (`hints3d/effects3d.ts`). Grep for the name before removing a token.

### Contrast

The 3D kit's pairs are measured in the `:root` comment of `style.css` (ink 7.7:1 on the rows; the result board's light text 11:1 on its plate). The Nov 15 tables for the blue HTML panels went with them (Nov 18); they are in the git history of this file.

### Space

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|

| `--tap` | 44 px | minimum touch target: every transparent button over a 3D drawing keeps it on touch (`pointer: coarse`) |
| `--slot-w` | `min(672px, 35vw)` | bottom unit panels (the lab kit's 35% of 1920) |

| `--inset-t` `--inset-r` `--inset-b` `--inset-l` | `--inset` + `env(safe-area-inset-*)` | the same margin clear of the notch and home bar; `.overlay` pads by the safe areas too (Nov 16) |
| `--lay-u` `--lay-x` `--lay-y` | one px of the 1920x1080 reference, the frame's margins | where a piece sits (`ui3d/layout.ts stage`) |

### Motion

The 3D pieces move on springs on the battle clock (`ui3d/spring.ts`) and draw only while something moves; Reduce motion stills them (`layer.still`). The CSS keeps one animation, the speaker portrait's `face-pop` (220 ms), off with Reduce motion. The speaker's mouth follows the typing (`mouthFrame` in `talk.ts`, 180 ms per frame). Battle timing (step, jump, strike) lives in `timing.ts`, never in CSS. With `prefers-reduced-motion`, nothing animates; in battle the strong strike does not shake the screen and the action camera stays off (Nov 16).

### Focus

- 3 px ring in `--focus`, offset 3 px, on every button, form field (text, number, select, checkbox, `details` summary) and the volume bar (`:focus-visible`).
- On the blue panels the yellow ring alone exceeds 6:1. **Outside the panels** (the corner buttons over the scene, title language button, the town's action balloon, dialogue box) `--focus-halo` is added: a dark band hugging the ring, so it shows on the bright sky (18.8:1) and on the grass.

- A dialog that rebuilds itself after an action (the Party screen) returns focus to the button with the same name (`aria-label`, else its text), else to its first control: by position it landed on another button once a row came or went (Nov 16).

### Marks on the board: four styles (Options, `?marks=`, Nov 17)

The tile marks, cursor, unit rings, range ring, map border, charge and counter lines and the shot preview are drawn in
one of four styles: `simples` (the flat drawing), `luz` (default: chamfered strips and glass that glow), `pedra` (stone
curbs and metal rings with reflection and cast shadow) and `neon` (tubes with a halo). `src/client/mark-style.ts` holds
the choice (`dt:marks`, `?marks=`; a player option) and
`src/client/scene/mark-kit.ts` the pieces. Rules that hold in every style:

- The colours are the ones of `MARK` (`scene/mark-looks.ts`) and the team colours; a style changes the material, never

## 8. Touch and mobile rules (Nov 16)

Written on Nov 16 from the touch audit (headless Chrome with touch emulated, `isMobile` and `hasTouch`, at 820×1180, 1180×820 and 390×844 on master `1c63faf`; every flow driven with taps only; details in `_notas/auditoria/D-toque-mobile.md`, outside git). Fernando reported that the tablet could not be played. Fernando's decision (Nov 16): phone and tablet are also a priority, so these rules apply to every new or changed screen. Each rule says **Holds today**, **Holds in part** or **Target**; a rule's breaks are its debt until fixed. Rules 1 to 9 were rechecked against master `62314ea` on Nov 16 (what was measured and what only read from code is marked on each).

**Nov 18 (Fernando):** a phone plays on its side only, the interface scales with the viewport (pieces and 3D text may go under 44 px and 16 px on a phone; 44 px is the goal for the hit area), the 3D layer keeps to the safe area, Undo is a button on screen and two fingers twisting turn the camera. Those rules and what they change in rules 1, 4, 6, 7 and 9 below are in [mobile-ux.md](../mobile-ux.md); the measures in [mobile-ux-auditoria.md](../mobile-ux-auditoria.md).

1. **Every target is at least 44 × 44 px** (Holds today). Buttons, menu rows, list buttons, icon buttons, form fields (a checkbox sits inside a label of at least 44 px) and links inside text (padded to 44 px of height). The size is the box the finger can hit (`getBoundingClientRect`), not the drawn glyph; two targets closer than 8 px count as one. Use `--tap`, never a literal under it. *Holds (measured Nov 16 on master `62314ea`, touch emulated, 820×1180 and 390×844):* no visible button under 44 px on the title, the town, the expedition gate and a battle. *Not remeasured since the first audit:* the windows opened from them (Shop, Tavern, Party, Options, Developer's checkboxes).
2. **The map is the one exception, covered by two taps** (Holds today). A half tile is smaller than a finger (about 32 × 22 px at the default zoom on 1180×820, measured), so a tap never acts at once on the map: the first selects the tile and shows what a mouse hover shows (path, strike preview, counter note, tile line), a second tap on the same tile confirms (`main.ts` tap handler, `interaction.ts`). Pinch zooms the camera.
3. **Every action the keyboard needs to finish a turn or leave a screen has a visible control** (Holds today). Esc, B and the right button are shortcuts; a long press or a two-finger gesture is never the only way. *Holds (read in code):* Back sits beside the ☰ on touch while there is something to go back to (`hud.ts` `renderBack`, `cancel()`); a conversation has a Skip button (`.talk-skip`, 44 px); the fps counter is an item of the ☰ menu (`hud-tools.ts`), off by default.
4. **Text is at least 16 px everywhere, touch included** (Holds today; Fernando, Nov 16: `--fs-xs` and `--fs-min` went from 12 and 14 to 16, `.kbd .hint` follows `--fs-sm`; since Nov 17 the hint is a drawn cap 1.75 em tall, its key letters 16.8 px, the letters on a pad's shoulder buttons 14 px as part of the drawing, and on the 3D layer a cap is never under 22 px, which leaves its letters at 13 px on a 1280x720 page). Body stays at `--fs` and notes at `--fs-sm`; the key hint and the key chip hide on touch. *Holds (measured Nov 16 on master `62314ea`):* no visible text under 16 px on the title, the town, the expedition gate and a battle at 820×1180 and 390×844.
5. **Contrast: 4.5:1 for text and 3:1 for marks** over the worst background the piece can sit on (section 9). A disabled control keeps its reason readable in a line outside the faded control ("20 short" pattern, holds in Shop and Tavern). *Breaks (computed from the tokens, not rechecked):* the fps counter over a white sky 4.1:1 (off by default). (Fixed on Nov 16: a closed expedition place is no longer a faded button, its reason is plain text in the place box; the enemy's ◆ is `--enemy-hi`.)
6. **Safe areas** (Holds today). `index.html` sets `viewport-fit=cover`; every piece anchored to an edge adds `env(safe-area-inset-*)` through the `--inset-t/r/b/l` tokens (`style.css` `:root`) and `.overlay` pads by the same. *Not tested on a device with a notch.*
7. **Both orientations work; nothing locks the screen** (Holds today). The layout follows width and height, never a device name; every window fits the screen or scrolls inside itself (`max-height` in `dvh`, `overflow: auto`); the page never scrolls sideways (`scrollWidth` equals `innerWidth` at 820×1180 and 390×844, measured on `62314ea`). A phone held upright shows a "Rotate the phone" note (`.rotate-note`, `rotate-note.ts` called from `entry.ts` since Nov 18, so the title and the town show it too; only `(pointer: coarse)`, up to 600 px wide and portrait); drawn in 3D on every layer once one exists (`ui3d/rotate3d.ts`), the HTML line unseen under it; a tablet is never asked to rotate. The expedition map's chart scrolls inside its own well, and its window scrolls with the page on a phone; How to play opens at its top (Nov 16).
8. **Nothing lives only in hover** (Holds in part). Every `:hover` style sits inside `@media (hover: hover)` (all nine, checked in `style.css`); information in a `title` tooltip also shows on screen or is not needed; copy that names a key or the mouse has a touch wording picked by the same `KEYS` test as the hints. *Holds:* the conversation hint, the facing and Move help lines and the Options controls have a touch wording. *Breaks (read):* `title`-only hints (hit chance ⊕, "Round N" in the turn list, icon buttons); key wording on touch in the town prompt ("W A S D walk…") and "Town menu (Esc)" (`game/town-hud.ts`).
9. **Gestures** (Holds in part). One finger dragging on the map pans the camera past an 8 px slop (`TAP_SLOP`, `touch-input.ts`), two fingers zoom the camera, a tap selects; `body` has `touch-action: none` and `user-select: none`, buttons `touch-action: manipulation`; every scrolling box has `touch-action: pan-y` and `html`, `body` have `overscroll-behavior: none` (`style.css`). *Not remeasured:* whether a pinch over How to play still zooms the page (it zoomed from 1× to 5× before `pan-y` was added). Finger scrolling worked in the turn list, Options, How to play, Shop and Party in the first audit.
10. **Screen bands.** A new rule picks one of the breakpoints the CSS already uses instead of a new number:

    | Band | Size | What changes today |
    |---|---|---|
    | Phone | up to 600 px wide | the Equipment window's slots on top (`.menu-layout` in one column) |
    | Narrow | up to 760 px wide | the Party screen's unit list goes on top of its page |
    | Narrow tablet | up to 900 px wide | the Result board's window goes under the hero rows; town windows open over the town menu with a veil (side by side needs 901 px); the title's rows may wrap |
    | Tablet landscape and small PC | 901 to 1599 px wide | town windows beside the menu; battle menus at the compact size (44 px rows) |
    | PC | 1600 px wide and up | the lab kit's full sizes (60 px menu rows, command menu 422 px); the Party screen's Equipment page puts the open slot beside the slots |
    | Short screen | up to 700 px tall | the title's foot follows the menu |

    *Target (not in the CSS):* a phone band for landscape under 500 px tall; every battle piece with a fixed width (the strike strips) takes `min(<width>, 100vw - 2 × var(--inset))`; a piece sized in `vw` has a floor that keeps its content readable. *Breaks (measured on `1c63faf`, not remeasured):* (the "Execute?" and submenu breaks went with those pieces, Nov 19) the unit panel at 35vw leaves its HP and CT bars 131 px wide at 1180, 5 px at 820 and 2 px at 390, and at 390 the two panels overlap.
11. **A phone's corners are fixed** (Holds for the command menu; Fernando, Nov 20, option A, "todos devem ficar presos"). On a phone (`phonePage`: a finger and a shorter side under 600 px) every battle piece keeps its own corner and none lies over another: the turn list on the left edge, the acting sheet bottom left, the tile line and Options top right, the command menu bottom right, standing on the combat bar's foot at the page's right edge with 44 px rows where the page has the height (`cornerLayout`, `menu-layout.ts`, tested at 844x390 and 667x375). A second panel replaces or sits beside, never on top: the Agir list replaces the menu in the same box (no cascade), the action's sheet sits left of the columns, and the target's plate in that corner shows only on the tile screens, where the menu is closed. *Target:* the turn list as a narrow column of portrait chips (today its cards keep their life and CT lines).
12. **How to test a touch change.**
    - Emulate a touch device, not only a size: Playwright with `isMobile: true, hasTouch: true` (a resized desktop window keeps `(hover: hover)` and `(pointer: fine)`). Before measuring, assert that `matchMedia('(hover: none)')` and `matchMedia('(pointer: coarse)')` match and that `.hud` has no `kbd` class.
    - Sizes: 820×1180 and 1180×820 (tablet), 390×844 (phone), 360×640 for the smallest phone.
    - Drive the flow with `touchscreen.tap` only: title, New game, town, a talk, the town menu, the quest, a room, Move, Act, a target, an action, a tile, Wait, the facing. A step that needs a key breaks rule 3.
    - Scroll with a real finger drag (`Input.dispatchTouchEvent` through CDP), not by setting `scrollTop`.
    - Measure every visible `button, a, input, select, summary, [role=button], [tabindex]` with `getBoundingClientRect` (rule 1), every visible text's computed `font-size` (rule 4) and `document.documentElement.scrollWidth` against `innerWidth` (rule 7).
    - Sound off: launch Chrome with `--mute-audio` and leave the music parameter out; any value of `?music` (or the old `?musica`, even `?musica=0`) turns off the automation mute (`AUTOMATED` in `sound.ts`).
    - Emulation does not show iPadOS Safari: no `contextmenu` on a long press, no focus on a tapped button, sticky hover, audio that unlocks only at the end of a tap. A touch change is done only after one pass on a real iPad (Safari) or Android tablet (Chrome).

## 9. Accessibility (Nov 16)

What the code does on master today, and the gaps the audits found (computed or read on Nov 16, not seen on screen unless said). A new piece keeps every "holds" below.

- **Focus is always visible** (holds). A 3 px `--focus` ring, offset 3 px, on `button`, `input`, `select` and `summary` (`:focus-visible`); outside the panels the dark `--focus-halo` hugs it (corner buttons, title language button, dialogue box); menu rows show focus as the band and the ▶, text and mark besides colour. A window opens with the focus inside it, `#app` behind is `inert`, and the Sheet returns the focus to the same button after an action. *Gaps:* the town's action balloon (`.town-act`, a button over the scene) has no halo, so its ring falls to 1.4:1 on a white sky; links (`.dialog a`, Credits) have no `:focus-visible` rule and keep the browser's default ring (not measured); on the title, closing a window does not return the focus to the row that opened it (Nov 16 windows review).
- **Reduced motion** (Options → Video and effects → Reduce motion: System (default), Yes, No; System follows `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, Yes forces it, No cancels the system's request; `motion.ts`, `dt:motion`, `data-motion` on `<html>`). Every CSS animation in Motion stops, the confetti does not fall, the battle behind the title stops turning, the painted mouth stays still and each sentence of the dialogue box shows whole instead of typing (`charMs` in `talk.ts`, Nov 18), the camera shake, the action camera's zoom and turn, the death slow motion and the recoil, bounce, dust and sparks of a hit are off, and the damage number stands still (`steps(1, end)` over 1.6 s). *Gap:* the hit-stop pause stays (a pause, not motion).
- **Reduced transparency** (`prefers-reduced-transparency: reduce`). Master reads it nowhere: panels and windows are opaque, and the translucent pieces are veils and bands whose text was measured over its worst background (`--strike` at 82%, the ability banner at 88%, the fps band at 55%, the window veil). Rule: a new translucent surface ships with an opaque fallback under this query and its contrast computed at its most transparent setting over a white sky (Panel glass, under Themes, does both in its branch). The turn list's see-through cards (Nov 22) fade to 66% (`SEE`, `turn3d/card.ts`) and stay opaque under this query; their light ink keeps 4.5:1 on the faded ground over the lightest lit snow of Frost Pass (`#d0cab4`), computed in `tests/queue-charge.test.ts`.
- **Contrast minimum.** Text 4.5:1; large text (24 px, or 18.66 px bold) and non-text marks 3:1; over the worst background the piece can sit on (the white sky for anything over the scene); colour is never the only cue. Measured pairs are in section 1. *Gaps (computed from `:root`):* the corner menu's key hint on a hovered row, 4.3:1 (`--ink-muted` on `--row-hi`; the Party row and the window list already turn their muted text `--ink`); the fps counter over a white sky, 4.1:1; the title's foot (`--fg` at 0.85 opacity on the radial veil), about 4.2:1 (the veil's opacity there is a deduction from the gradient); the enemy's side stripe and face frame, 2.6:1 on the panel blue (decorative, and the word ENEMY carries the side; the ◆ itself is `--enemy-hi`, 4.1:1, since Nov 16). A disabled control is exempt from the bar, so its reason goes in text outside it (Disabled).
- **Text size.** 16 px minimum on a PC and 14 px on a touch screen, with the exceptions listed under Typography; the interface has no text size setting.
- **Names and roles** (holds, read). Icon buttons are named by `title` and `aria-label`; decorative symbols and menu heads are `aria-hidden` (the menu's `aria-label` names it); the corner buttons are named buttons (Options, Undo, Back, Turn the camera left or right); `choice()` uses `aria-pressed`; the objective and ability banners are `role="status"`, the help line and the dialogue text `aria-live="polite"`. Never checked with a screen reader.
